1 · Gauss (+ superposition)

  • $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec a=Q_{enc}/\epsilon_0$. Pick a sphere, a coaxial cylinder or a pillbox so $E$ is constant and $\parallel d\vec a$.
  • Uniform ball: $\vec E=\frac{\rho}{3\epsilon_0}\vec r$ inside, $\frac{kQ}{r^2}\hat r$ outside. $V_{in}=\frac{kQ}{2R^3}(3R^2-r^2)$, so $V(0)=\tfrac32V(R)$.
  • Infinite cylinder: $\vec E=\frac{\rho}{2\epsilon_0}s\,\hat s$ inside, $\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0 s}\hat s$ outside, with $\lambda=\rho\pi a^2$. Line: $V=-\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s}{s_0}$. Only $\Delta V$ (it diverges at $\infty$).
  • Shell: $E=0$ inside. Slab/sheet: $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$. Conductor surface: $\sigma/\epsilon_0$.
  • Hole = $+\rho$ + $(-\rho)$. Use each piece's field from its own center/axis, then add the vectors.
  • Spherical cavity in a ball, offset $\vec d$: $\vec E=\frac{\rho}{3\epsilon_0}\vec d$ (uniform). In a cylinder it is not uniform: $\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}(\frac x6,\frac y6,-\frac z3)$.
  • Ring (axis): $E_z=\frac{kqz}{(z^2+R^2)^{3/2}}$. Disk: $E_z=\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}\big(1-\frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2+R^2}}\big)$, $V=\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}(\sqrt{z^2+R^2}-z)$.

2 · Given E (or V), find ρ: check the origin

  • $\rho=\frac{\epsilon_0}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(r^2E_r)$ for $r\neq0$. Then $Q_{enc}(r)=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$.
  • If $\lim_{r\to0}4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r=q\neq0$: point charge, $\rho\supset q\,\delta^3(\vec r)$. ($E\sim1/r^2$ near 0 ⇒ there is a δ.)
  • $\nabla\cdot\frac{\hat r}{r^2}=4\pi\delta^3(\vec r)$, $\nabla^2\frac1r=-4\pi\delta^3(\vec r)$, Poisson $\nabla^2V=-\rho/\epsilon_0$.
  • $Q_{tot}=\lim_{r\to\infty}4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$. Check: $Q_{tot}=q+\int\rho\,d\tau$ (+ any $\sigma$ from jumps in $E_r$).
  • $V=\frac{Ae^{-\lambda r}}{r}$ ⇒ $\rho=\epsilon_0A\big[4\pi\delta^3(\vec r)-\lambda^2\frac{e^{-\lambda r}}{r}\big]$.
  • $E\propto r^{-4}$ all the way to $r=0$ ⇒ infinite charge (not a δ). A sensible version has $1/r^4$ only for $r>R$, or a mix like $\frac A{r^2}+\dots$
  • $\int f\delta(x-a)dx=f(a)$, $\delta(kx)=\delta(x)/|k|$. Shell: $\rho=\sigma\,\delta(r-R)$.

3 · Boundary conditions (number them!)

  • Across $\sigma$: $E^\perp_{above}-E^\perp_{below}=\sigma/\epsilon_0$. $E^\parallel$ and $V$ continuous. $\frac{\partial V_{out}}{\partial n}-\frac{\partial V_{in}}{\partial n}=-\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$.
  • Conductor: $E=0$ and $\rho=0$ inside, $V$ const, $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\frac{\partial V}{\partial n}$, $\vec E_{out}=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}\hat n$, pressure $\frac{\sigma^2}{2\epsilon_0}$.
  • Checklist: (1) each surface: $V$ given, or $V$=const with $Q$ given. (2) Finite at $r=0$ / $s=0$. (3) $V\to0$ at $\infty$ (or $\to-E_0r\cos\theta$). (4) Continuity + jump at each shell. (5) Symmetry.
  • "Grounded" = $V=0$. "Isolated, neutral" = $V$ unknown const + $\oint\sigma=0$. "Held at $V_0$" = battery: $Q$ free.
  • Uniqueness: $V$ (or $Q$ on each conductor) on every boundary + $\rho$ ⇒ unique. Any guess that fits is the answer.
  • Laplace: no max/min inside, $V(\text{center})$ = average over a sphere. Earnshaw: no stable equilibrium.

4 · Images

  • Plane, $q$ at height $d$: $-q$ at $-d$. $\sigma=\frac{-qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$, total $-q$; $F=\frac{kq^2}{(2d)^2}$ (attract). Half the charge lies within $s=\sqrt3d$.
  • Energy = half of the real + image pair: $W=-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$. Work to pull $q$ off: $+\frac{kq^2}{4d}$.
  • 90° corner: 3 images ($-q,-q,+q$). Wedge $\pi/n$: $2n-1$. Two parallel planes: ∞ row. Line $\lambda$ over a plane: $-\lambda$.
  • Grounded sphere ($q$ at $a>R$): $q'=-\frac Ra q$ at $b=\frac{R^2}{a}$. $F=\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$ (attract).
    $\sigma(\theta)=\frac{-q(a^2-R^2)}{4\pi R(R^2+a^2-2aR\cos\theta)^{3/2}}$, total $-qR/a$.
  • Twists = add a charge at the center: held at $V_0$: $+4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$. Neutral: $+\frac Raq$. Total charge $Q$: $Q+\frac Raq$.
  • $q$ inside a shell at distance $d$: image $-\frac Rdq$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$ (outside). Shell at $V_0$: just add $V_0$ inside (no force change).
  • Image valid only in the region of interest (outside the metal). Images never sit in that region.

5 · Energy, capacitors

  • $W=\tfrac12\sum q_iV_i$ ($V$ from the others) $=\tfrac12\int\rho V d\tau=\frac{\epsilon_0}2\int E^2d\tau$. Not superposable (cross terms).
  • Uniform ball: $\frac35\frac{kQ^2}{R}$. Shell: $\frac12\frac{kQ^2}{R}$. Pair: $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$.
  • $C=Q/\Delta V$, $W=\tfrac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$. Plates $\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$; spheres $\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0ab}{b-a}$; coax $\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\ln(b/a)}$.

6 · Separation: Cartesian

  • Direction with two zero BCs ⇒ $\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$ (oscillates); the other ⇒ $e^{\pm kx}$, $\sinh$, $\cosh$, with $k=\frac{n\pi}a$.
  • Open to $\infty$: $e^{-kx}$. Zero at $x=b$: $\sinh k(b-x)$. Zero at 0: $\sinh kx$. Symmetric: $\cosh k(x-\frac b2)$.
  • Fourier: $\int_0^a\sin\frac{n\pi y}a\sin\frac{m\pi y}a dy=\frac a2\delta_{nm}$. $C_n=\frac2a\int_0^aV_0(y)\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}dy$. Const $V_0$: $\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$, odd $n$.
  • Live face at $x=b$: $V=\sum C_n\sin\frac{n\pi y}a\frac{\sinh(n\pi x/a)}{\sinh(n\pi b/a)}$. Several live faces: superpose one per face.
  • Slot: $V=\frac{4V_0}\pi\sum_{odd}\frac1ne^{-n\pi x/a}\sin\frac{n\pi y}a=\frac{2V_0}\pi\tan^{-1}\frac{\sin(\pi y/a)}{\sinh(\pi x/a)}$.
  • 3-D: $\sin\sin\times\sinh(\pi\sqrt{n^2/a^2+m^2/b^2}\,z)$; const: $C_{nm}=\frac{16V_0}{\pi^2nm}$ (odd, odd).
  • Series → $V_0/2$ at a discontinuity; Gibbs overshoot only in the partial sum.

7 · Separation: polar (2-D, no z)

  • $\nabla^2=\frac1s\partial_s(s\partial_s)+\frac1{s^2}\partial_\phi^2$. Try $R=s^\lambda$ ⇒ $\lambda=\pm k$.
  • $V=a_0+b_0\ln s+\sum_k\big[s^k(a_k\cos k\phi+b_k\sin k\phi)+s^{-k}(c_k\cos k\phi+d_k\sin k\phi)\big]$.
  • Full circle ⇒ $k$ integer, no $\phi$ term. Origin in region ⇒ drop $s^{-k}$ and $\ln s$. $\infty$ ⇒ drop $s^k$ ($\ln s$ ↔ net $\lambda$).
  • Wedge walls $\theta=0,\alpha$ at 0: $\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}\alpha$, $k=\frac{n\pi}\alpha$. Zero arc at $r=a$: $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$. Live arc $r=b$ at $V_0$: $C_n=\frac{2V_0(1-(-1)^n)}{n\pi[(b/a)^k-(a/b)^k]}$ (Spring '26).
  • One wall at $V_0$, the other 0, no arcs: $V=V_0\theta/\alpha$. Tip field $\propto r^{\pi/\alpha-1}$ (blows up if $\alpha>\pi$).
  • Pipe in $E_0\hat x$: $V=-E_0(s-\frac{R^2}s)\cos\phi$, $\sigma=2\epsilon_0E_0\cos\phi$.

8 · Separation: spherical / Legendre

  • $V=\sum(A_\ell r^\ell+\frac{B_\ell}{r^{\ell+1}})P_\ell(\cos\theta)$. Inside: $A$ only. Outside: $B$ only (+ $-E_0r\cos\theta$). $r^{-(\ell+1)}$, not $r^{-\ell}$.
  • $P_0=1$, $P_1=x$, $P_2=\frac{3x^2-1}2$, $P_3=\frac{5x^3-3x}2$, $P_4=\frac{35x^4-30x^2+3}8$. $P_\ell(1)=1$, $P_\ell(-1)=(-1)^\ell$, $P_2(0)=-\frac12$, $P_4(0)=\frac38$.
  • $x^2=\frac13+\frac23P_2$, $x^3=\frac35P_1+\frac25P_3$, $x^4=\frac15+\frac47P_2+\frac8{35}P_4$, $\cos2\theta=-\frac13+\frac43P_2$, $\sin^2\theta=\frac23-\frac23P_2$.
  • $\int_{-1}^1P_\ell P_m dx=\frac{2\delta_{\ell m}}{2\ell+1}$ ⇒ $c_\ell=\frac{2\ell+1}2\int_{-1}^1f(x)P_\ell dx$ ($dx=\sin\theta d\theta$).
  • Shell at $V_0(\theta)=\sum c_\ell P_\ell$: in $c_\ell(\frac rR)^\ell$, out $c_\ell(\frac Rr)^{\ell+1}$. $\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0}R\sum(2\ell+1)c_\ell P_\ell$, $Q=4\pi\epsilon_0Rc_0$.
  • Given $\sigma=\sum\sigma_\ell P_\ell$: $A_\ell=\frac{\sigma_\ell}{(2\ell+1)\epsilon_0R^{\ell-1}}$, $B_\ell=A_\ell R^{2\ell+1}$.
  • Metal sphere in $E_0\hat z$: $V=-E_0(r-\frac{R^3}{r^2})\cos\theta$, $\sigma=3\epsilon_0E_0\cos\theta$ (+$\frac{RV_0}r$ if at $V_0$).
  • Axis trick: know $V(z)$ on the axis, expand in $z^\ell$ or $z^{-(\ell+1)}$, replace by $r^\ell P_\ell$ or $r^{-(\ell+1)}P_\ell$.
  • $\frac1{|\vec r-\vec r'|}=\sum\frac{r_<^\ell}{r_>^{\ell+1}}P_\ell(\cos\gamma)$.

9 · Recipes (most of the points)

  • Separation: (1) region + coordinates; (2) numbered BCs; (3) product ansatz, sign of the constant (oscillate where two BCs are zero); (4) apply the zero BCs ⇒ quantized $k$, basis; (5) superpose; (6) Fourier/Legendre on the live boundary; (7) check a limit, a symmetry, a corner value.
  • Images: (1) region of interest; (2) BCs on the metal; (3) images outside the region; (4) verify every BC (then uniqueness ⇒ done); (5) $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial V/\partial n$; (6) force = images on $q$ only; (7) energy = half.
  • Given E, find ρ and V: divergence for $r\neq0$, flux of a tiny sphere at 0, flux at ∞, jumps in $E_r$ ⇒ $\sigma$; $V$ by integrating in from ∞ piece by piece.
  • Superposed Gauss: each piece's field from its own center, then add vectors; $\Delta V$ by integrating each piece.

10 · Classic results

  • Shell $\sigma=k\cos\theta$: $V_{in}=\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}r\cos\theta$ (uniform $\vec E=-\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}\hat z$); outside a pure dipole $p=\frac43\pi R^3k$.
  • Nested metal shells: inner surface carries $-q_{enc}$, outer surface carries $Q_{shell}+q_{enc}$. A cavity's field depends only on the charge inside it.
  • Edge at $V_0\frac ya$: $C_n=\frac{2V_0(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}$. Const: $\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$ (odd). $\int_0^a\sin^2=\frac a2$.
  • Line $\lambda$ at height $d$ over a grounded plane: $V=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s_-}{s_+}$ ($s_\pm$ = distance to $\pm\lambda$).
  • Sphere at $V_0$ + grounded-sphere image pair: $V=$ image solution $+\frac{RV_0}{r}$ outside; inside a shell at $V_0$: image solution $+V_0$.

11 · Which method? Traps

  • High symmetry ⇒ Gauss. Charge + flat/spherical metal ⇒ images. Box/slot ⇒ Cartesian. Sphere with $V(\theta)$ or $\sigma(\theta)$ ⇒ Legendre. Wedge/pipe ⇒ polar.
  • Setup gets most of the points: region, numbered BCs, general solution first.
  • $d\tau=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$; $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ (metal) vs $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$ (sheet); images half energy; check units and limits.
  • $\int\frac{dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$, $\int\frac{x\,dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{-1}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$, $\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}=\ln(x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2})$, $\int_0^\pi\sin^3=\frac43$, $\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\cos^2\theta=\frac23$.
