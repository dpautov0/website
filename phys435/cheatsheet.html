PHYS 435 · Exam I cheat sheet · FRONT: equations + concepts$k\equiv\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ · grey = how to derive it

1 · Gauss + superposition

  • $\oint\vec E\cdot d\vec a=Q_{enc}/\epsilon_0$. Sphere / coaxial cylinder / pillbox so $E$ is constant and $\parallel d\vec a$.
  • Ball: $\vec E=\frac{\rho}{3\epsilon_0}\vec r$ in, $\frac{kQ}{r^2}\hat r$ out. $V_{in}=\frac{kQ}{2R^3}(3R^2-r^2)$, $V(0)=\tfrac32V(R)$.
  • Cylinder: $\frac{\rho}{2\epsilon_0}s\,\hat s$ in, $\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0s}\hat s$ out ($\lambda=\rho\pi a^2$). Line: $V=-\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s}{s_0}$; only $\Delta V$.
  • Shell: $E=0$ in. Sheet: $\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$. Metal surface: $\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$.
  • Hole = $+\rho$ plus $-\rho$. Each piece's field from its own center/axis; add vectors.
  • Ball cavity offset $\vec d$: $\vec E=\frac{\rho}{3\epsilon_0}\vec d$ uniform. Spherical cavity in a cylinder: $\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}(\frac x6,\frac y6,-\frac z3)$, not uniform.
  • Ring axis $E_z=\frac{kqz}{(z^2+R^2)^{3/2}}$. Disk $V=\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}(\sqrt{z^2+R^2}-z)$.

2 · Delta functions, E → ρ

  • $\int f\,\delta(x-a)dx=f(a)$, $\delta(kx)=\frac{\delta(x)}{|k|}$, $\delta^3(\vec r)$ has units 1/vol. Shell: $\rho=\sigma\,\delta(r-R)$.
  • $\nabla\cdot\frac{\hat r}{r^2}=4\pi\delta^3(\vec r)$, $\nabla^2\frac1r=-4\pi\delta^3(\vec r)$. Flux of $\hat r/r^2$ through any sphere is $4\pi$, but the divergence is 0 for $r\ne0$, so it is all at the origin.
  • $\rho=\frac{\epsilon_0}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(r^2E_r)$ ($r\ne0$). $Q_{enc}(r)=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$.
  • Origin: $\lim_{r\to0}4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r=q\ne0$ ⇒ $+q\,\delta^3(\vec r)$. (→0: none. →∞: infinite charge, unphysical.)
  • Jump in $E_r$ at $R$ ⇒ $\sigma=\epsilon_0(E_{out}-E_{in})$. $Q_{tot}=\lim_{\infty}4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$ = point + volume + surface.
  • Poisson $\nabla^2V=-\rho/\epsilon_0$. $V=\frac{Ae^{-\lambda r}}{r}$ ⇒ $\rho=\epsilon_0A[4\pi\delta^3-\lambda^2\frac{e^{-\lambda r}}{r}]$.

3 · Boundary conditions

  • $E^\perp_{above}-E^\perp_{below}=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$; $E^\parallel$ and $V$ continuous. $\frac{\partial V_{out}}{\partial n}-\frac{\partial V_{in}}{\partial n}=-\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$. Pillbox: flux $(E_a-E_b)A=\sigma A/\epsilon_0$. Thin loop: $\oint E\cdot dl=0$ ⇒ $E^\parallel$ same.
  • Conductor: $E=0$, $\rho=0$ inside, $V$ const, $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\frac{\partial V}{\partial n}$, $\vec E_{out}=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}\hat n$, pressure $\frac{\sigma^2}{2\epsilon_0}$.
  • Words: grounded ⇒ $V=0$. Isolated neutral ⇒ $V$ = unknown const, $\oint\sigma=0$. Held at $V_0$ ⇒ battery, $Q$ free. Far away ⇒ $V\to0$ or $\to-E_0r\cos\theta$. Insulated wall ⇒ $\partial V/\partial n=0$.
  • Always also: finite at $r=0$ / $s=0$; continuity + jump at every shell.
  • Count: each region's unknown constants = number of conditions.

3b · Conductors (the review's 7 facts) + capacitance

  • (1) $\vec E=0$ inside; (2) $\rho=0$ inside; (3) net charge only on the surface; (4) equipotential; (5) $\vec E\perp$ surface just outside, $=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}\hat n$; (6) empty cavity: $\vec E=0$ inside it, even in an external field; (7) charge $q$ in a cavity ⇒ $-q$ on the cavity wall, $+q$ (+ any net $Q$) on the outside: the conductor "looks charged".
  • $C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}$ (geometry only). Plates $\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$; spheres $\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0ab}{b-a}$ (isolated: $4\pi\epsilon_0R$); coax $\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\ln(b/a)}$. Recipe: put $\pm Q$, Gauss for $E$, $\Delta V=-\int E\cdot dl$, divide.

4 · Laplace, uniqueness

  • $\nabla^2V=0$ where $\rho=0$: no local max/min; $V(\text{center})$ = average over any sphere/circle. Earnshaw: no stable equilibrium in empty space.
  • 1st theorem: $\rho$ inside + $V$ on every boundary ⇒ unique. 2nd: conductors with given $Q$ each ⇒ $\vec E$ unique.
  • So any guess (images, separated series) that satisfies Laplace + every BC is the answer. Proof: difference $V_3=V_1-V_2$ obeys Laplace with 0 on boundary ⇒ no max/min ⇒ $V_3=0$.

5 · Images

  • Plane, $q$ at $d$: $-q$ at $-d$. $\sigma=\frac{-qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$, total $-q$, half within $s=\sqrt3d$. $F=\frac{kq^2}{(2d)^2}$ (attract).
  • Energy: $W=-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$ = half the 2-charge value (field only exists on one side). Work to pull off: $+\frac{kq^2}{4d}$.
  • Corner 90°: images $-q,-q,+q$. Wedge $\pi/n$: $2n-1$ images ($180°/\alpha$ must be an integer). Two parallel planes: infinite row. Line $\lambda$: image $-\lambda$.
  • Grounded sphere: $q'=-\frac Raq$ at $b=\frac{R^2}{a}$. Derive: $V=0$ at the near point $a-R$ and far point $a+R$ ⇒ 2 equations. $F=\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$.
  • $\sigma(\theta)=\frac{-q(a^2-R^2)}{4\pi R(R^2+a^2-2aR\cos\theta)^{3/2}}$, total $-\frac{qR}{a}$.
  • Twists: add charge at the center. At $V_0$: $+4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$. Neutral: $+\frac{qR}{a}$. Net $Q$: $Q+\frac{qR}{a}$.
  • $q$ inside a shell at $d$: image $-\frac Rdq$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$ (outside). At $V_0$: add $V_0$ inside.
  • Valid only in the region of interest; images never sit in it.

6 · Energy

  • $W=\frac12\sum q_iV_i$ ($V$ from the others) $=\frac12\int\rho V\,d\tau=\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\int E^2d\tau$ (last includes self-energy). Not superposable.
  • Ball $\frac35\frac{kQ^2}{R}$, shell $\frac12\frac{kQ^2}{R}$, pair $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$. $C=\frac{Q}{\Delta V}$, $W=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$; plates $\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$, spheres $\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0ab}{b-a}$.
  • Force: $F=-\frac{dW}{dx}$ at fixed $Q$. At fixed $V$ the battery does work: $F=+\frac{dW}{dx}$.

7 · Separation: Cartesian

  • $V=X(x)Y(y)$ ⇒ $\frac{X''}X+\frac{Y''}Y=0$ ⇒ $X''=k^2X$, $Y''=-k^2Y$.
  • Direction with two zero BCs oscillates: $\sin\frac{n\pi y}a$, $k=\frac{n\pi}a$. Other direction: $e^{\pm kx}$, $\sinh$, $\cosh$.
  • To ∞: $e^{-kx}$. Zero at $x=b$: $\sinh k(b-x)$. Zero at 0: $\sinh kx$. Symmetric: $\cosh k(x-\frac b2)$. Insulated walls: $\cos$, plus a $k=0$ term.
  • Fourier: $\int_0^a\sin\frac{n\pi y}a\sin\frac{m\pi y}a dy=\frac a2\delta_{nm}$ ⇒ $C_n=\frac2a\int_0^aV_0(y)\sin\frac{n\pi y}ady$. Const: $\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$ odd $n$. Linear $V_0\frac ya$: $\frac{2V_0(-1)^{n+1}}{n\pi}$.
  • Several live faces: one problem per face, add. 3-D: $\sin\sin\sinh(\pi\sqrt{\frac{n^2}{a^2}+\frac{m^2}{b^2}}z)$, const $C_{nm}=\frac{16V_0}{\pi^2nm}$.
  • Series → $V_0/2$ at a jump; Gibbs overshoot only in partial sums.

8 · Separation: polar (2-D, no $z$)

  • $\nabla^2=\frac1s\partial_s(s\partial_s)+\frac1{s^2}\partial_\phi^2$. Try $R=s^\lambda$: $s(sR')'=\lambda^2R$ ⇒ $\lambda=\pm k$.
  • $V=a_0+b_0\ln s+\sum[s^k(a_k\cos k\phi+b_k\sin k\phi)+s^{-k}(c_k\cos k\phi+d_k\sin k\phi)]$.
  • Full circle ⇒ $k$ integer, no $\phi$ term. Origin in region: drop $s^{-k}$, $\ln s$. $\infty$: drop $s^k$ ($\ln s$ = net $\lambda$).
  • Wedge walls 0 at $\theta=0,\alpha$: $\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}\alpha$, $k=\frac{n\pi}\alpha$. Zero arc at $a$: $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$. Fourier: $\frac2\alpha\int_0^\alpha$.
  • One wall $V_0$, other 0, no arcs: $V_0\theta/\alpha$. Tip $|E|\propto r^{\pi/\alpha-1}$ (dies if $\alpha\lt \pi$, blows up if $\alpha>\pi$).
  • Pipe in $E_0\hat x$: $-E_0(s-\frac{R^2}{s})\cos\phi$, $\sigma=2\epsilon_0E_0\cos\phi$.

9 · Separation: spherical / Legendre

  • $V=\sum(A_\ell r^\ell+\frac{B_\ell}{r^{\ell+1}})P_\ell(\cos\theta)$. Try $r^n$: $n(n+1)=\ell(\ell+1)$ ⇒ $n=\ell$ or $-(\ell+1)$. Not $r^{-\ell}$.
  • Inside: $A$ only. Outside: $B$ only (+$-E_0r\cos\theta$). Shell region $a\lt r\lt b$: both.
  • $P_0=1$, $P_1=x$, $P_2=\frac{3x^2-1}2$, $P_3=\frac{5x^3-3x}2$, $P_4=\frac{35x^4-30x^2+3}8$; $P_\ell(1)=1$, $P_\ell(-1)=(-1)^\ell$, $P_2(0)=-\frac12$.
  • $x^2=\frac13+\frac23P_2$, $x^3=\frac35P_1+\frac25P_3$, $\cos2\theta=-\frac13+\frac43P_2$, $\sin^2\theta=\frac23-\frac23P_2$.
  • $c_\ell=\frac{2\ell+1}2\int_{-1}^1f(x)P_\ell dx$ ($x=\cos\theta$). Shell at $V_0(\theta)=\sum c_\ell P_\ell$: in $c_\ell(\frac rR)^\ell$, out $c_\ell(\frac Rr)^{\ell+1}$; $\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0}R\sum(2\ell+1)c_\ell P_\ell$; $Q=4\pi\epsilon_0Rc_0$.
  • $\sigma=\sum\sigma_\ell P_\ell$ given: $A_\ell=\frac{\sigma_\ell}{(2\ell+1)\epsilon_0R^{\ell-1}}$, $B_\ell=A_\ell R^{2\ell+1}$.
  • Axis trick: $V(z)$ on axis → expand in $z^\ell$ or $z^{-(\ell+1)}$ → replace with $r^\ell P_\ell$, $r^{-(\ell+1)}P_\ell$.
  • Total $Q$ only from $\ell=0$; $\vec E(0)$ only from $\ell=1$; far field from lowest nonzero $\ell$.

Recipes (setup is most of the points)

  • Which method: symmetry ⇒ Gauss; charge + flat/round metal ⇒ images; box/slot ⇒ Cartesian; long wedge/pipe ⇒ polar; sphere with $V(\theta)$ or $\sigma(\theta)$ ⇒ Legendre; $\vec E$ given ⇒ divergence + origin check.
  • Separation: (1) region; (2) numbered BCs; (3) ansatz + sign of constant; (4) zero BCs ⇒ basis, quantized $k$; (5) superpose; (6) Fourier/Legendre on the live boundary; (7) check a limit / symmetry / corner.
  • Images: region; BCs on the metal; place images outside the region; verify every BC ⇒ uniqueness; $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial_nV$; force = images on $q$; energy = half.
  • Write partial results even if stuck: BC list + general solution earn most of the credit.

Ex 1 · Given $\vec E$, find $\rho$ and $V$

$\vec E=\frac{A}{r^2}\hat r$ ($r\lt R$), $\frac{AR^2}{r^4}\hat r$ ($r>R$).

  • $r\lt R$: $r^2E_r=A$ const ⇒ $\rho=0$ for $r\ne0$, but $4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r\to4\pi\epsilon_0A$ ⇒ $\rho\supset4\pi\epsilon_0A\,\delta^3(\vec r)$.
  • $r>R$: $\rho=\frac{\epsilon_0}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\frac{AR^2}{r^2}=-\frac{2\epsilon_0AR^2}{r^5}$. At $R$: $E$ continuous ⇒ $\sigma=0$.
  • Check: cloud $=\int_R^\infty\rho\,4\pi r^2dr=-4\pi\epsilon_0A$ ⇒ total 0, matches $r^2E\to0$.
  • $V_{out}=\int_r^\infty E=\frac{AR^2}{3r^3}$; $V_{in}=V(R)+\int_r^RE=\frac Ar-\frac{2A}{3R}$.

Ex 2 · Superposed Gauss

Infinite cylinder radius $a$ (+ρ, along $z$) through a ball radius $R>a$ (−ρ).

  • Pieces: cyl $\frac{\rho}{2\epsilon_0}(x,y,0)$ inside; ball $-\frac{\rho}{3\epsilon_0}(x,y,z)$ inside.
  • Overlap: $\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}(\frac x6,\frac y6,-\frac z3)$; check $\nabla\cdot\vec E=0$ ✓.
  • $z=0$, $a\lt s\lt R$: $E_s=\frac{\rho a^2}{2\epsilon_0s}-\frac{\rho s}{3\epsilon_0}$, zero at $s=\sqrt{3/2}\,a$.
  • Axis $z>R$: cylinder gives 0 ⇒ $E_z=-\frac{\rho R^3}{3\epsilon_0z^2}$.

Ex 3 · Charge over a grounded plane

  • BCs: $V=0$ on $z=0$; $V\to0$ far. Image $-q$ at $z=-d$.
  • $V=kq\big[\frac{1}{\sqrt{s^2+(z-d)^2}}-\frac{1}{\sqrt{s^2+(z+d)^2}}\big]$, $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial_zV|_0=\frac{-qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$.
  • $\int\sigma\,da=-q$. $F=\frac{kq^2}{4d^2}$ down. $W=\int_\infty^dF\,dz=-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$ (half of $-\frac{kq^2}{2d}$).

Ex 4 · Sphere held at $V_0$ + charge $q$ at $a$

  • BCs: (1) $V=V_0$ on $r=R$; (2) $V\to0$ at ∞.
  • Images: $q'=-\frac{qR}a$ at $\frac{R^2}a$, plus $q_0=4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$ at the center ($V_0$ everywhere on the sphere).
  • $Q_{sphere}=4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0-\frac{qR}{a}$; $\sigma$ = grounded $\sigma$ + $\frac{\epsilon_0V_0}{R}$.
  • $F=\frac{qRV_0}{a^2}-\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$ (+ = away). $F=0$ at $a=2R$ ⇒ $V_0=\frac89\frac{kq}{R}$; unstable.

Ex 5 · Cartesian slot

Plates $y=0,a$ grounded; end $x=0$ at $V_0$; open to $x\to\infty$.

  • BCs: (1) $V(x,0)=0$ (2) $V(x,a)=0$ (3) $V\to0$, $x\to\infty$ (4) $V(0,y)=V_0$.
  • (1)(2) ⇒ $\sin\frac{n\pi y}a$; (3) ⇒ $e^{-n\pi x/a}$; (4) Fourier ⇒ $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$ odd.
  • $V=\frac{4V_0}\pi\sum_{odd}\frac1ne^{-n\pi x/a}\sin\frac{n\pi y}a=\frac{2V_0}{\pi}\tan^{-1}\frac{\sin(\pi y/a)}{\sinh(\pi x/a)}$.
  • Closed at $x=b$ (grounded): replace $e^{-kx}$ by $\frac{\sinh k(b-x)}{\sinh kb}$.

Ex 6 · Polar wedge (Spring '26 P1)

$a\lt r\lt b$, $0\lt \theta\lt \alpha$; walls + inner arc grounded, $r=b$ at $V_0$.

  • BCs: $R(a)=0$, $\Theta(0)=0$, $\Theta(\alpha)=0$, $\Phi(b,\theta)=V_0$.
  • $\Theta''=-k^2\Theta$ ⇒ $\sin k\theta$, $k=\frac{n\pi}\alpha$; $R=Cr^k+Dr^{-k}$, $R(a)=0$ ⇒ $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$.
  • $\Phi=\sum C_n\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}\alpha[(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k]$, $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi[(b/a)^k-(a/b)^k]}$, odd $n$.
  • Polar = Cartesian with $u=\ln r$: the bracket is $2\sinh(k\ln\frac ra)$.

Ex 7 · Shell at $V_0\cos^2\theta$ (Legendre)

  • $\cos^2\theta=\frac13P_0+\frac23P_2$.
  • $V_{in}=V_0[\frac13+\frac23\frac{r^2}{R^2}P_2]$, $V_{out}=V_0[\frac{R}{3r}+\frac23\frac{R^3}{r^3}P_2]$.
  • $\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0V_0}{R}[\frac13+\frac{10}3P_2]$, $Q=\frac43\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$. $\vec E(0)=0$ (no $\ell=1$).

Ex 8 · Metal sphere in $E_0\hat z$

  • BCs: $V(R)=0$; $V\to-E_0r\cos\theta$ far.
  • Far field is pure $\ell=1$ ⇒ $V=-E_0r\cos\theta+\frac{B_1\cos\theta}{r^2}$; $V(R)=0$ ⇒ $B_1=E_0R^3$.
  • $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial_rV|_R=3\epsilon_0E_0\cos\theta$. Held at $V_0$: add $\frac{RV_0}{r}$. Pipe version: $R^2/s$, factor 2.

Ex 9 · Shell with $\sigma=k\cos\theta$

  • BCs: finite at 0; $\to0$ at ∞; $V$ continuous; $\partial_rV_{out}-\partial_rV_{in}=-\frac{k\cos\theta}{\epsilon_0}$.
  • $V_{in}=Ar\cos\theta$, $V_{out}=\frac{AR^3\cos\theta}{r^2}$; jump: $-2A-A=-\frac{k}{\epsilon_0}$ ⇒ $A=\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}$.
  • Inside: uniform $\vec E=-\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}\hat z$.

Ex 10 · Energy of a uniform ball, two ways

  • $\frac{\epsilon_0}2\int E^2$: $\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\big[\int_0^R(\frac{kQr}{R^3})^2+\int_R^\infty(\frac{kQ}{r^2})^2\big]4\pi r^2dr=\frac35\frac{kQ^2}R$.
  • $\frac12\int\rho V$ with $V_{in}=\frac{kQ}{2R^3}(3R^2-r^2)$: same.

Ex 11 · Neutral isolated sphere + $q$ at $a$

  • BCs: $V$ = const (unknown) on the sphere; $\oint\sigma=0$; $V\to0$ far.
  • Images: $-\frac{qR}a$ at $\frac{R^2}a$ (makes $V=0$) and $+\frac{qR}a$ at the center (restores $Q=0$, keeps $V$ const).
  • $F=\frac{kq^2R}{a}\big[\frac{a^2}{(a^2-R^2)^2}-\frac1{a^2}\big]$ toward the sphere; sphere's $V=\frac{kq}{a}$.

Ex 12 · Charge in a 90° grounded corner

  • $q$ at $(x_0,y_0)$; walls $x=0$, $y=0$. Images: $-q$ at $(-x_0,y_0)$, $-q$ at $(x_0,-y_0)$, $+q$ at $(-x_0,-y_0)$.
  • $F_x=kq^2\big[-\frac1{4x_0^2}+\frac{x_0}{4(x_0^2+y_0^2)^{3/2}}\big]$ (same with $x\leftrightarrow y$). Valid only in $x,y>0$.

Ex 13 · Charge inside a grounded shell

  • $q$ at $d\lt R$: image $-\frac{qR}{d}$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$ (outside). $F=\frac{kq^2Rd}{(R^2-d^2)^2}$ toward the nearest wall.
  • Inner surface carries $-q$ total. Shell at $V_0$: same inside plus $V_0$ (same force).

Ex 14 · Box with two live faces

  • Square pipe $0\le x,y\le a$: $y=0$ and $y=a$ at $V_0$, sides grounded.
  • Sides zero ⇒ $\sin\frac{n\pi x}a$. Symmetric in $y$ ⇒ $\cosh\frac{n\pi(y-a/2)}{a}$. $C_n\cosh\frac{n\pi}2=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$ (odd).
  • Center: by symmetry $V_0/2$ (this + the rotated problem = all faces at $V_0$).

Classic results to quote

  • Nested metal shells: inner surface $-q_{enc}$, outer $Q_{shell}+q_{enc}$. A cavity's field depends only on its own charge.
  • Line $\lambda$ at $d$ over a grounded plane: $V=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s_-}{s_+}$.
  • $\frac1{|\vec r-\vec r'|}=\sum\frac{r_\lt ^\ell}{r_>^{\ell+1}}P_\ell(\cos\gamma)$ (on the axis this is the axis trick).

Traps + integrals

  • $d\tau=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$; $\sigma/\epsilon_0$ (metal) vs $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$ (sheet); $r^{-(\ell+1)}$; images = half energy; sinh in the non-zero direction; check units and limits.
  • $\int\frac{dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$, $\int\frac{x\,dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{-1}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$, $\int\frac{dx}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}=\ln(x+\sqrt{x^2+a^2})$, $\int\frac{dx}{x^2+a^2}=\frac1a\tan^{-1}\frac xa$.
  • $\int_0^\pi\sin^3=\frac43$, $\int_0^\pi\sin\theta\cos^2\theta=\frac23$, $\int_0^a\sin^2\frac{n\pi y}a=\frac a2$, $\int_0^a\sin\frac{n\pi y}a\,dy=\frac{a(1-(-1)^n)}{n\pi}$.
