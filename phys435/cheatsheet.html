PHYS 435 · Exam I · FRONT (most important first)$k=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ · all fields are magnitudes; direction in words

1 · Set up first (most of the points)

  • Write: region, coordinates, numbered BCs, general solution. Then solve.
  • Grounded: $V=0$. Held at $V_0$: $V=V_0$. Isolated + neutral: $V$ = unknown const, total charge 0. Far away: $V\to0$ (uniform field: $V\to-E_0r\cos\theta$).
  • Always: $V$ finite at the center; $V$ continuous everywhere.
  • Across a charged surface: $E_\perp$ jumps by $\sigma/\epsilon_0$. Same thing: $\partial_nV_{out}-\partial_nV_{in}=-\sigma/\epsilon_0$.
  • On metal: $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\,\partial V/\partial n$. Field just outside $=\sigma/\epsilon_0$, straight out.

2 · Method of images

  • Plane, $q$ at height $d$: image $-q$ at depth $d$. Force $\frac{kq^2}{(2d)^2}$ toward plane. Energy $-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$ (half the pair).
  • $\sigma=-\frac{qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$, total $-q$.
  • Grounded sphere $R$, $q$ at distance $a$: image $q'=-\frac{qR}{a}$ at $\frac{R^2}{a}$ from center. Force $\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$ (pull).
  • Twist = extra charge at the center: held at $V_0$ → $4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$; neutral → $+\frac{qR}{a}$; charge $Q$ → $Q+\frac{qR}{a}$.
  • $q$ inside grounded shell at $d$: image $-\frac{qR}{d}$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$ (outside).
  • 90° corner: 3 images ($-q,-q,+q$). Line charge: image $-\lambda$.
  • Only valid outside the metal. Check every BC → uniqueness says it's right.

3 · Separation: boxes (Cartesian)

  • $V=X(x)Y(y)$. Direction with two zero walls gets $\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$.
  • Other direction: $e^{-n\pi x/a}$ (open to ∞), $\sinh\frac{n\pi(b-x)}{a}$ (zero at $x=b$), $\cosh$ (symmetric).
  • $C_n=\frac2a\int_0^aV_0(y)\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}dy$. Constant $V_0$: $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$, odd $n$ only.
  • Live face at $x=b$: divide by $\sinh\frac{n\pi b}{a}$. Two live faces: solve each, add.
  • At a jump in the edge value, the series gives the average.

4 · Separation: spheres (Legendre)

  • $V=\sum\big(A_\ell r^\ell+\frac{B_\ell}{r^{\ell+1}}\big)P_\ell(\cos\theta)$.
  • Inside: only $A_\ell r^\ell$. Outside: only $\frac{B_\ell}{r^{\ell+1}}$. Not $r^{-\ell}$.
  • $x=\cos\theta$: $P_0=1$, $P_1=x$, $P_2=\frac{3x^2-1}{2}$, $P_3=\frac{5x^3-3x}{2}$. Every $P_\ell(1)=1$.
  • $\cos^2\theta=\frac13+\frac23P_2$. $\cos^3\theta=\frac35P_1+\frac25P_3$.
  • Anything else: $c_\ell=\frac{2\ell+1}{2}\int_{-1}^{1}f(x)P_\ell(x)dx$.
  • Shell at $V(\theta)=\sum c_\ell P_\ell$: inside $c_\ell(\frac rR)^\ell P_\ell$, outside $c_\ell(\frac Rr)^{\ell+1}P_\ell$, $\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0}{R}\sum(2\ell+1)c_\ell P_\ell$.
  • Shell with $\sigma=\sum\sigma_\ell P_\ell$: inside $A_\ell=\frac{\sigma_\ell}{(2\ell+1)\epsilon_0R^{\ell-1}}$, outside $B_\ell=A_\ell R^{2\ell+1}$.
  • Metal sphere in uniform $E_0$ (up): $V=-E_0\big(r-\frac{R^3}{r^2}\big)\cos\theta$, $\sigma=3\epsilon_0E_0\cos\theta$.

5 · Separation: wedges and pipes (polar)

  • Long in $z$. $\Theta=\sin k\theta,\cos k\theta$; $R=r^k,\ r^{-k}$ ($k=0$: $\ln r$, $\theta$).
  • Walls at $\theta=0,\alpha$ grounded: $\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}{\alpha}$, $k=\frac{n\pi}{\alpha}$.
  • Has the center: only $r^k$. Goes to ∞: only $r^{-k}$. Zero arc at $a$: $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$.
  • Full circle: $k=1,2,3,\dots$ One wall at $V_0$, other 0, no arcs: $V=V_0\theta/\alpha$.

6 · Conductors and capacitors

  • Inside metal: $E=0$, no charge, one value of $V$. Charge sits on the surface.
  • Empty cavity: $E=0$ inside it. Charge $q$ in cavity: $-q$ on the cavity wall, $+q$ (+ net $Q$) outside.
  • $C=Q/\Delta V$. Plates $\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d}$. Spheres $\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0ab}{b-a}$. One sphere $4\pi\epsilon_0R$. Coax $\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0L}{\ln(b/a)}$. Energy $\frac12CV^2$.

7 · Gauss fields (magnitudes, outward)

  • Outside a ball or point: $\frac{kQ}{r^2}$. Inside uniform ball: $\frac{\rho r}{3\epsilon_0}$. Inside a shell: 0.
  • Line: $\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0s}$. Inside uniform cylinder: $\frac{\rho s}{2\epsilon_0}$.
  • Sheet: $\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$ each side. Metal surface: $\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$.
  • Hole = full shape + a negative piece. Each field from its own center, then add.
  • Ball potential inside: $V=\frac{kQ(3R^2-r^2)}{2R^3}$.

8 · Given E, find ρ (delta at the origin)

  • Away from 0: $\rho=\frac{\epsilon_0}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}(r^2E)$.
  • Charge inside radius $r$: $4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E$. If it tends to $q\ne0$ as $r\to0$: point charge $q\,\delta^3$ at the origin.
  • $E$ jumps at $R$: $\sigma=\epsilon_0(E_{out}-E_{in})$. Total: $4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E$ as $r\to\infty$.
  • $\nabla^2\frac1r=-4\pi\delta^3$. $\int f(x)\delta(x-a)dx=f(a)$.

9 · Energy

  • Charges: $W=\frac12\sum q_iV_i$ ($V$ from the others). Spread out: $W=\frac12\int\rho V=\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\int E^2$.
  • Ball $\frac35\frac{kQ^2}{R}$. Shell $\frac12\frac{kQ^2}{R}$. Pair $\frac{kq_1q_2}{r}$. Not additive.

10 · Laplace and uniqueness

  • No charge: $\nabla^2V=0$. No max or min inside; $V$ = average over a sphere around the point.
  • $V$ given on every boundary (or $Q$ on each metal) → only one answer. Anything that fits is right.

11 · Traps + integrals

  • $d\tau=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$. Metal $\sigma/\epsilon_0$, sheet $\sigma/2\epsilon_0$. Use $\cos\theta$ inside $P_\ell$.
  • $\int\frac{dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{x}{a^2\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$. $\int\frac{x\,dx}{(x^2+a^2)^{3/2}}=\frac{-1}{\sqrt{x^2+a^2}}$.
  • $\int_0^a\sin^2\frac{n\pi y}{a}dy=\frac a2$. $\int_0^\pi\sin^3\theta\,d\theta=\frac43$.

Ex 1 · Slot (Cartesian)

  • BCs: (1) $V=0$ at $y=0$; (2) $V=0$ at $y=a$; (3) $V\to0$ as $x\to\infty$; (4) $V=V_0$ at $x=0$.
  • (1)(2) → $\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$. (3) → $e^{-n\pi x/a}$. (4) → $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$, odd $n$.
  • $V=\frac{4V_0}{\pi}\sum_{odd}\frac1n e^{-n\pi x/a}\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$.
  • Closed at $x=b$: use $\frac{\sinh(n\pi(b-x)/a)}{\sinh(n\pi b/a)}$ instead.

Ex 2 · Box, top and bottom at $V_0$

  • Sides zero → $\sin\frac{n\pi x}{a}$. Symmetric top/bottom → $\cosh\frac{n\pi(y-a/2)}{a}$.
  • $C_n\cosh\frac{n\pi}{2}=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$, odd $n$.
  • Center: $V_0/2$ (this + the turned-sideways problem = every face at $V_0$).

Ex 3 · Wedge (Spring '26 exam)

  • $a\lt r\lt b$. BCs: $V=0$ on both walls and on $r=a$; $V=V_0$ on $r=b$.
  • Walls → $\sin\frac{n\pi\theta}{\alpha}$, $k=\frac{n\pi}{\alpha}$. Zero at $a$ → $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$.
  • $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi[(b/a)^k-(a/b)^k]}$, odd $n$.

Ex 4 · Shell at $V_0\cos^2\theta$

  • $\cos^2\theta=\frac13+\frac23P_2$.
  • Inside: $V_0\big[\frac13+\frac23\frac{r^2}{R^2}P_2\big]$.
  • Outside: $V_0\big[\frac{R}{3r}+\frac23\frac{R^3}{r^3}P_2\big]$.
  • $\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0V_0}{R}\big[\frac13+\frac{10}{3}P_2\big]$.

Ex 5 · Metal sphere in a uniform field

  • BCs: $V=0$ on sphere; far away $V\to-E_0r\cos\theta$.
  • Only $\ell=1$: $V=-E_0r\cos\theta+\frac{B\cos\theta}{r^2}$. $V(R)=0$ → $B=E_0R^3$.
  • $\sigma=3\epsilon_0E_0\cos\theta$: + on top, − on bottom.

Ex 6 · Shell with $\sigma=k\cos\theta$

  • Inside $Ar\cos\theta$; outside $\frac{AR^3\cos\theta}{r^2}$ (matches at $R$).
  • Jump: $-2A-A=-\frac{k}{\epsilon_0}$ → $A=\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}$.
  • Inside: uniform field $\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}$ pointing down.

Ex 7 · Charge over a grounded plane

  • $V=kq\big[\frac1{\text{dist to }q}-\frac1{\text{dist to image}}\big]$.
  • $\sigma=-\frac{qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$, total $-q$; half lies within $s=\sqrt3d$.
  • Force $\frac{kq^2}{4d^2}$ down. Energy $-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$.

Ex 8 · Sphere held at $V_0$, charge $q$ at $a$

  • Images: $q'=-\frac{qR}{a}$ at $\frac{R^2}{a}$, plus $q_0=4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$ at center.
  • Sphere's charge: $q_0-\frac{qR}{a}$.
  • Force on $q$: $\frac{qRV_0}{a^2}$ (push) $-\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$ (pull).
  • Zero at $a=2R$ when $V_0=\frac89\frac{kq}{R}$. Unstable.

Ex 9 · Neutral sphere, charge $q$ at $a$

  • Images: $-\frac{qR}{a}$ at $\frac{R^2}{a}$ and $+\frac{qR}{a}$ at center.
  • Always pulls $q$ in. Sphere's $V=\frac{kq}{a}$.

Ex 10 · Charge in a 90° corner

  • $q$ at $(x_0,y_0)$. Images: $-q$ at $(-x_0,y_0)$, $-q$ at $(x_0,-y_0)$, $+q$ at $(-x_0,-y_0)$.
  • Force: add the three pulls/pushes as vectors.

Ex 11 · Charge inside a grounded shell

  • Image $-\frac{qR}{d}$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$ (outside).
  • Force $\frac{kq^2Rd}{(R^2-d^2)^2}$ toward the near wall. Inner wall holds $-q$.

Ex 12 · Given E, find ρ and V

$E=\frac{A}{r^2}$ inside $R$, $\frac{AR^2}{r^4}$ outside, outward.

  • Inside: $r^2E=A$ constant → no $\rho$ for $r>0$; point charge $4\pi\epsilon_0A$ at origin.
  • Outside: $\rho=-\frac{2\epsilon_0AR^2}{r^5}$. $E$ continuous at $R$ → $\sigma=0$. Total 0.
  • $V_{out}=\frac{AR^2}{3r^3}$, $V_{in}=\frac{A}{r}-\frac{2A}{3R}$.

Ex 13 · Cylinder through a ball

Cylinder $+\rho$ (radius $a$, along $z$), ball $-\rho$ (radius $R$).

  • Inside both: sideways $\frac{\rho s}{6\epsilon_0}$ out, along $z$: $\frac{\rho z}{3\epsilon_0}$ toward center.
  • Plane $z=0$, $a\lt s\lt R$: $\frac{\rho a^2}{2\epsilon_0s}-\frac{\rho s}{3\epsilon_0}$, zero at $s=1.22a$.

Ex 14 · Energy of a uniform ball

  • Inside $E=\frac{kQr}{R^3}$, outside $\frac{kQ}{r^2}$.
  • $\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\int E^2\,4\pi r^2dr=\frac35\frac{kQ^2}{R}$.
