A2★ Recipe: Gauss & D → E → P

1. Symmetry sentence: "by spherical/cyl./planar symmetry $\mathbf D=D(r)\hat r$".

2. Surface through the point: sphere $4\pi r^2$ · cylinder $2\pi sL$ · pillbox $A$ (2A for a free sheet).

3. $D\cdot$area $=Q_{f,enc}$; nonuniform: $\int\rho\,4\pi r'^2dr'$, $\int\rho\,2\pi s'L\,ds'$.

4. $E=D/\epsilon$ per region → $P=D-\epsilon_0E$ → $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\hat n$, $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$.

5. $V$ from ∞ inward, one region at a time.

6. Check: $\sum q_b=0$; E cont. where σ=0; εr→1.

No dielectric: same with $\epsilon_0E$ and total Q.

A3★ Recipe: Laplace boundary-value problem

1. Regions + coordinates (sphere r,θ · cyl s,φ · box x,y).

2. General solution in each region (F-group).

3. Kill terms: center finite ⇒ no $r^{-(l+1)}$, $s^{-n}$, $\ln s$; ∞ ⇒ no $r^l$ (except $-E_0r\cos\theta$).

4. Number BCs: (i) V cont. (ii) $\epsilon\partial_nV$ jump $=-\sigma_f$ (iii) V on conductor (iv) far field.

5. Expand given $V_0(\theta)$/σ(θ) in $P_l$; match term by term.

6. $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$; σ; check limits (εr→1, ∞).

Uniform field ⇒ only l=1 (n=1). Polynomial in cosθ ⇒ finite l.

A4★ Recipe: images

1. Put image charges outside the region you solve in.

2. Check V on every boundary (0 or $V_0$) and at ∞ ⇒ uniqueness ⇒ done.

3. $V$ = real + images; $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial_nV$; induced total = Σ images (grounded).

4. Force on q: Coulomb from images only. Energy: ½(q·V of images).

Twists: plane at $V_0$: +V₀. Sphere at $V_0$: +$4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$ at center. Neutral sphere: +$\frac{qR}a$ at center. Dielectric: ×$\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+1}$, below use $q''$.

A5★ Recipe: given a field → everything

1. Read components + variables; use the one-liner (H-group).

2. $\rho=\epsilon_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf E$ (or $\rho_f=\nabla\cdot\mathbf D$, $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$). Electrostatic needs $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$.

3. Jumps at R: $\sigma=\epsilon_0(E^\perp_{out}-E^\perp_{in})$.

4. Delta: $Q_{enc}=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$ as r→0 ≠ 0 ⇒ point charge.

5. V: integrate in from ∞; verify $-\nabla V$.

6. Energy: shells; outside a sphere $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}$; with dielectric ½∫D²/ε.

B · Electrostatics basics (Exam I)

B1EXAM I · electrostatics core

$\mathbf E=k\int\frac{dq\,\hat{\mathscr r}}{\mathscr r^2}$ · $V=k\int\frac{dq}{\mathscr r}$ · $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$ · $V(b)-V(a)=-\int_a^b\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$ · $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac\rho{\epsilon_0}$ · $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$ · $\nabla^2V=-\frac\rho{\epsilon_0}$ · $W=q\Delta V$

BC: $E^\perp_{out}-E^\perp_{in}=\frac\sigma{\epsilon_0}$, $E^\parallel$ cont., V cont.; $\partial_nV_{out}-\partial_nV_{in}=-\frac\sigma{\epsilon_0}$.

Conductors: E=0 inside, V const, charge on surface; just outside $E=\frac\sigma{\epsilon_0}\hat n$; $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial_nV$; pressure $\frac{\sigma^2}{2\epsilon_0}$. Empty cavity: E=0. q in cavity: $-q$ on wall, $+q$(+Q) outside, outside field indep. of q's position. Grounded: V=0; isolated: Q fixed, V unknown const.

Given E → ρ: $\rho=\epsilon_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf E$; $Q_{enc}(r)=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$; $Q_{enc}\to q\ne0$ as $r\to0$ ⇒ $q\delta^3$ at origin; E jump at R ⇒ $\sigma=\epsilon_0(E_{out}-E_{in})$. $\int f\delta(x-a)=f(a)$, $\delta(kx)=\frac{\delta(x)}{|k|}$, $\delta^3(\mathbf r)=\frac{\delta(r)}{4\pi r^2}$. Ex: $E=\frac A{r^2}$ in, $\frac{AR^2}{r^4}$ out ⇒ $4\pi\epsilon_0A\,\delta^3$, $\rho_{out}=-\frac{2\epsilon_0AR^2}{r^5}$, $V_{in}=\frac Ar-\frac{2A}{3R}$, $V_{out}=\frac{AR^2}{3r^3}$.

Laplace: no max/min inside; $V(\mathbf r)$ = average over any sphere around it. Uniqueness: V given on all boundaries (or Q on each conductor + V at ∞) ⇒ unique; anything that fits is THE answer.

Energy: $W=\frac12\sum q_iV_i=\frac12\int\rho V=\frac{\epsilon_0}2\int E^2$; pair $\frac{kq_1q_2}r$; shell $\frac{kQ^2}{2R}$, ball $\frac{3kQ^2}{5R}$; not additive (cross term $\epsilon_0\int\mathbf E_1\cdot\mathbf E_2$).

C · Multipoles & dipoles

C1Multipole expansion

$V=k\sum_n\frac1{r^{n+1}}\int r'^nP_n(\cos\alpha)\rho\,d\tau'$ = $k[\frac Qr+\frac{\mathbf p\cdot\hat r}{r^2}+\frac{1}{r^3}\sum q_ir_i^2P_2(\cos\alpha_i)+\dots]$

$Q=\int\rho$, $\mathbf p=\int\mathbf r'\rho\,d\tau'=\sum q_i\mathbf r_i$; shift origin by $\mathbf a$: $\mathbf p'=\mathbf p-Q\mathbf a$ (Q=0 ⇒ p fixed).

n-th term: $V\propto r^{-(n+1)}$, $E\propto r^{-(n+2)}$; moment units C·mⁿ.

$P_0{=}1$, $P_1{=}x$, $P_2{=}\frac{3x^2-1}2$, $P_3{=}\frac{5x^3-3x}2$; $\int_{-1}^1P_lP_{l'}dx=\frac{2\delta_{ll'}}{2l+1}$; $P_l(1)=1$, $P_l(-1)=(-1)^l$.

Lin. quadrupole ($q,-2q,q$, spacing a): $V=\frac{kqa^2(3\cos^2\theta-1)}{r^3}$. Square alternating: Q=p=0.

$\sigma=k_0\cos\theta$ shell: $p=\frac43\pi R^3k_0$; $V_{in}=\frac{k_0}{3\epsilon_0}r\cos\theta$, $V_{out}=\frac{k_0R^3\cos\theta}{3\epsilon_0r^2}$.

D · Polarization, D, linear dielectrics

D1★ D → E → P chain (always this order)

1. $\oint\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf a=Q_{f,enc}$ (needs symmetry) · $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f$

2. $\mathbf E=\mathbf D/\epsilon$ (linear) · $\mathbf E=(\mathbf D-\mathbf P)/\epsilon_0$ (frozen P)

3. $\mathbf P=\mathbf D-\epsilon_0\mathbf E=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\mathbf D=(1-\frac1{\epsilon_r})\mathbf D$

4. $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\hat n$ ($\hat n$ out of material; inner face $-\hat r$) · $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$ · $\sum q_b=0$

5. $V=-\int\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$ from ∞ inward, region by region

Linear homog.: $\rho_b=-\frac{\chi_e}{1+\chi_e}\rho_f$ · at conductor $\sigma_f+\sigma_b=\sigma_f/\epsilon_r$ · $q_b$ at point q $=-\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}q$

No free + symmetry ⇒ D=0 ⇒ E=−P/ε₀ in, 0 out. Trap: $\nabla\times\mathbf D=\nabla\times\mathbf P$; no symmetry ⇒ D not from $\rho_f$ alone. Free charge only on surface of dielectric ball ⇒ inside D=E=P=0.

D3Linear setups (free charge given)
