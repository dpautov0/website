1 · E, D, P: FIELDS IN DIELECTRICS
GENERAL SETUP
$\mathbf E$ = total field from ALL charge: $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac{\rho_f+\rho_b}{\epsilon_0}$, $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$, $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$
$\mathbf P$ = dipole moment per volume (C/m²)
Bound charge: $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\hat n$ ($\hat n$ out of material), $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$
$\mathbf D\equiv\epsilon_0\mathbf E+\mathbf P$ (C/m²): $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f$, $\oint\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf a=Q_{f,enc}$
$\nabla\times\mathbf D=\nabla\times\mathbf P$ (NOT zero in general)
USEFUL FACTS
Linear: $\mathbf P=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf D=\epsilon\mathbf E$
$\epsilon=\epsilon_0(1+\chi_e)=\epsilon_0\epsilon_r$, so $\epsilon_r=1+\chi_e$, $\chi_e=\epsilon_r-1$
Vacuum: $\chi_e=0$, $\epsilon_r=1$, $\epsilon=\epsilon_0$
$\mathbf P=\mathbf D-\epsilon_0\mathbf E=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\mathbf D=(1-\frac1{\epsilon_r})\mathbf D$
$\mathbf E=\mathbf D/\epsilon$ (linear); $\mathbf E=(\mathbf D-\mathbf P)/\epsilon_0$ (frozen P, no ε!)
Homogeneous linear: $\rho_b=-\frac{\chi_e}{1+\chi_e}\rho_f$ ⇒ no free charge inside ⇒ no $\rho_b$
Next to a conductor: $\sigma_f+\sigma_b=\sigma_f/\epsilon_r$
No free charge + symmetry ⇒ D = 0 ⇒ E = −P/ε₀ inside, 0 outside
Total bound charge always 0 (use as a check)
Recipe: D by Gauss (free only) → E = D/ε → P → σb, ρb → V from ∞ in
Typical εᵣ: air 1.0005, glass 4–7, Si 12, water 80
SPECIFIC RESULTS
1.1 Uniformly polarized sphere
$\sigma_b=P\cos\theta$, $\rho_b=0$
$\mathbf E_{in}=-\frac{\mathbf P}{3\epsilon_0}$ (uniform)
Outside: pure dipole $\mathbf p=\frac43\pi R^3\mathbf P$
$V_{in}=\frac{P}{3\epsilon_0}r\cos\theta$, $V_{out}=\frac{PR^3\cos\theta}{3\epsilon_0r^2}$
Why: = two balls ±ρ offset by d, ρd = P; E points + cap → − cap.
1.2 Ball with radial P = k rHW 4.10
$\rho_b=-3k$, $\sigma_b=kR$
$\mathbf E_{in}=-k\mathbf r/\epsilon_0$, $E_{out}=0$
General $P=kr^n\hat r$: $\rho_b=-(n+2)kr^{n-1}$, $\sigma_b=kR^n$
Why: D = 0 by symmetry; bound charge totals 0.
1.3 Shell a<r<b with P = (k/r) r̂Gr 4.15
$\rho_b=-k/r^2$
$\sigma_b(a)=-k/a$, $\sigma_b(b)=+k/b$
$E=-\frac{k}{\epsilon_0r}\hat r$ in shell, 0 elsewhere
$P=\frac k{r^2}\hat r$: $\rho_b=0$, $\sigma_b(a)=-\frac k{a^2}$, $\sigma_b(b)=\frac k{b^2}$
1.4 Long cylinder with P = k s ŝ
$\rho_b=-2k$, $\sigma_b=ka$, $E_{in}=-ks/\epsilon_0$
General $ks^n\hat s$: $\rho_b=-(n+1)ks^{n-1}$
Uniform P ⊥ axis: $\sigma_b=P\cos\phi$, $\mathbf E_{in}=-\mathbf P/2\epsilon_0$
1.5 Uniform P in a cylinder (bar electret)HW 4.11
$\sigma_b=\pm P$ on caps, 0 on sides
Long: $E_{in}\approx0$; like point charges $\pm P\pi a^2$
Short disk: $E_{in}\approx-P/\epsilon_0$ (capacitor)
Shape factor: $\mathbf E_{in}=-N\mathbf P/\epsilon_0$
N: slab ⊥ 1, sphere ⅓, cylinder ⊥ ½, needle ∥ 0
1.6 Slab with P = P₀(z/a) ẑ, |z|<a
$\rho_b=-P_0/a$
$\sigma_b=+P_0$ on both faces
$E=-\frac{P_0z}{a\epsilon_0}$ inside, 0 outside
1.7 Cavities and shells of uniform P
Uniformly polarized shell: E = 0 in the cavity
Spherical cavity in P-slab (P ⊥): $E=-\frac{2P}{3\epsilon_0}$
Outside that slab: dipole $-\frac43\pi R^3\mathbf P$
1.8 Point charge at center of a dielectric ball
(charge −Q, susceptibility χₑ, radius R)
$\mathbf D=-\frac{Q}{4\pi r^2}\hat r$
$\mathbf E=-\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0(1+\chi_e)r^2}\hat r$
$\mathbf P=-\frac{\chi_eQ}{4\pi(1+\chi_e)r^2}\hat r$
$\sigma_b(R)=-\frac{\chi_eQ}{4\pi(1+\chi_e)R^2}$
$\rho_b=0$ for r>0, plus point $+\frac{\chi_eQ}{1+\chi_e}$ at the origin
(from $\nabla\cdot\frac{\hat r}{r^2}=4\pi\delta^3$); outside $E=-\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon_0r^2}$
1.9 Conductor (Q, radius a) + dielectric shell to bHW 4.26
$D=\frac{Q}{4\pi r^2}$, $E=\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon r^2}$ in shell
$V(a)=kQ[\frac1b+\frac1{\epsilon_r}(\frac1a-\frac1b)]$
$\sigma_b(a)=-\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{Q}{4\pi a^2}$, $\sigma_b(b)=+\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{Q}{4\pi b^2}$
Two shells, at interface b: $\sigma_b=\frac{Q}{4\pi b^2}(\frac1{\epsilon_{r2}}-\frac1{\epsilon_{r1}})$
1.10 Coated wire λ / coax with dielectric
$D=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi s}$, $E=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon s}$
$\sigma_b(a)=-\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{\lambda}{2\pi a}$, $\sigma_b(b)=+\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{\lambda}{2\pi b}$
$V(a)-V(b)=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon}\ln\frac ba$
1.11 Ball of dielectric holding free ρf
$D=\frac{\rho_fr}{3}$ in, $\frac{Q}{4\pi r^2}$ out; $E_{in}=\frac{\rho_fr}{3\epsilon}$
$\rho_b=-\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\rho_f$, $\sigma_b=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{\rho_fR}{3}$
Free charge only on the surface ⇒ inside D = E = P = 0
1.12 Frozen P slab inside a capacitor (±σ)
$D=\sigma$ everywhere between plates
$E_{slab}=\frac{\sigma-P}{\epsilon_0}$, $E_{gap}=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$
$\Delta V=\frac{\sigma d-Pt}{\epsilon_0}$; $\sigma_b=\pm P$ on slab faces
2 · CAPACITORS & ENERGY
GENERAL SETUP
$C=Q/\Delta V$
$W=\frac12QV=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$
All charge: $W=\frac{\epsilon_0}2\int E^2d\tau=\frac12\int\rho V\,d\tau$
With dielectric (work on free charge): $W=\frac12\int\mathbf D\cdot\mathbf E\,d\tau=\frac12\int\frac{D^2}{\epsilon}d\tau$
USEFUL FACTS
Stacked layers = series (same D): $\frac1C=\sum\frac1{C_i}$
Side by side = parallel (same E): $C=\sum C_i$
Fill with εᵣ: fixed Q ⇒ W ÷ εᵣ; fixed V ⇒ W × εᵣ
Force: $F=\frac12V^2\frac{dC}{dx}$, toward higher C (fixed Q gives same F)
Conductor surface pressure: $\frac{\sigma^2}{2\epsilon_0}$
Outside a sphere the energy is always $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}$
Infinite line/cylinder: energy per length diverges outside
SPECIFIC RESULTS
2.1 Parallel plates, stacked slabsHW 4.18
$D=\sigma$ in both; $E_i=\sigma/\epsilon_i$; $P_i=\sigma(1-\frac1{\epsilon_{ri}})$
Interface: $\sigma_b=\sigma(\frac1{\epsilon_{r2}}-\frac1{\epsilon_{r1}})$
$\frac1C=\sum\frac{d_i}{\epsilon_iA}$; half-filled: $C=\frac{2\epsilon_r}{1+\epsilon_r}C_0$
4.18 (εᵣ 2, 1.5; thickness a): $V=\frac{7\sigma a}{6\epsilon_0}$
2.2 Parallel plates, side-by-side slabsDisc 4.19
$E=V/d$ in both; $\sigma_{f,i}=D_i=\epsilon_iE$
$C=\sum\frac{\epsilon_iA_i}{d}$; half-filled: $C=\frac{1+\epsilon_r}{2}C_0$
2.3 CoaxHW 4.21
$\frac CL=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0}{\sum\ln(b_i/a_i)/\epsilon_{ri}}$ (layers in series)
Half-filled by angle: $\frac CL=\frac{\pi\epsilon_0(1+\epsilon_r)}{\ln(b/a)}$
2.4 Spherical capacitor
$C=\frac{4\pi\epsilon ab}{b-a}$; isolated sphere $4\pi\epsilon_0R$
Layers: $\frac1C=\sum\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_i}(\frac1{a_i}-\frac1{b_i})$
$\epsilon=\epsilon_0\frac br$: $C=\frac{4\pi\epsilon_0b}{\ln(b/a)}$
2.5 Permittivity that varies
$D=\sigma$; $E=\sigma/\epsilon(x)$; $C=A/\int_0^d\frac{dx}{\epsilon}$; $\rho_b=-dP/dx$
$\epsilon=\epsilon_0(1+\frac xd)$: $C=\frac{\epsilon_0A}{d\ln2}$
Linear $\epsilon_1\to\epsilon_2$: $C=\frac{A(\epsilon_2-\epsilon_1)}{d\ln(\epsilon_2/\epsilon_1)}$
2.6 Dielectric pulled into a capacitor
Width w, gap d, inserted x: $C=\frac{\epsilon_0w}{d}(\ell+\chi_ex)$
$F=\frac{\epsilon_0\chi_ewV^2}{2d}$ (pulled in)
2.7 Oil rising in a coaxGr 4.28
$F=\frac{\pi\epsilon_0\chi_eV^2}{\ln(b/a)}$
$h=\frac{\epsilon_0\chi_eV^2}{\rho g(b^2-a^2)\ln(b/a)}$
2.8 Standard energies
Shell $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}$; uniform ball $\frac{3Q^2}{20\pi\epsilon_0R}$
Ball of εᵣ with ρf: $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}(1+\frac1{5\epsilon_r})$
Conductor + shell: $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r}(\frac1a+\frac{\chi_e}b)$
Coax per length: $\frac{\lambda^2}{4\pi\epsilon}\ln\frac ba$
3 · BOUNDARY CONDITIONS & LAPLACE PROBLEMS
GENERAL SETUP
No charge in a region ⇒ $\nabla^2V=0$
Sphere: $V=\sum(A_lr^l+\frac{B_l}{r^{l+1}})P_l(\cos\theta)$
Cylinder: $A_0+B_0\ln s+\sum(A_ns^n+B_ns^{-n})(C_n\cos n\phi+D_n\sin n\phi)$
Box: $e^{\pm kx}\sin ky$, $\sinh$, $\cosh$
$D_1^\perp-D_2^\perp=\sigma_f$; $\mathbf E^\parallel$ continuous; V continuous
No free σ: $\epsilon_1\partial_nV_1=\epsilon_2\partial_nV_2$
Charged sheet in vacuum: $\partial_nV_{out}-\partial_nV_{in}=-\sigma/\epsilon_0$
USEFUL FACTS
Center finite ⇒ drop $r^{-(l+1)}$, $s^{-n}$, $\ln s$
Far away ⇒ drop $r^l$, except $-E_0r\cos\theta$ for a uniform field
Uniform field ⇒ only l = 1 (n = 1) terms appear
$P_0=1$, $P_1=\cos\theta$, $P_2=\frac{3\cos^2\theta-1}{2}$, $P_3=\frac{5\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta}{2}$
$\cos^2\theta=\frac13+\frac23P_2$; $c_l=\frac{2l+1}2\int_{-1}^1f(x)P_l(x)dx$
Field lines bend: $\frac{\tan\theta_1}{\tan\theta_2}=\frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}$ (away from normal in high ε)
Uniqueness: anything that meets every BC is THE answer
Check every answer: εᵣ → 1 (vacuum), εᵣ → ∞ (conductor)
SPECIFIC RESULTS
3.1 Dielectric sphere in uniform E₀
$V_{in}=Ar\cos\theta$; $V_{out}=-E_0r\cos\theta+\frac{B\cos\theta}{r^2}$
BCs at R: $AR=-E_0R+\frac{B}{R^2}$ and $\epsilon_rA=-E_0-\frac{2B}{R^3}$
$E_{in}=\frac{3E_0}{\epsilon_r+2}$ uniform; $B=\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0R^3$
$P=3\epsilon_0\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0$; $\sigma_b=P\cos\theta$
Just outside the pole: $E=\frac{3\epsilon_r}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0$
3.2 Conducting sphere in uniform E₀
$V=-E_0(r-\frac{R^3}{r^2})\cos\theta$
$\sigma=3\epsilon_0E_0\cos\theta$; induced $p=4\pi\epsilon_0R^3E_0$
With net charge Q: add $\frac{kQ}{r}$
Conducting cylinder ⊥: $V=-E_0(s-\frac{R^2}{s})\cos\phi$, $\sigma=2\epsilon_0E_0\cos\phi$
3.3 Bubble, or any εin inside εout
Sphere: $E_{in}=\frac{3\epsilon_{out}}{\epsilon_{in}+2\epsilon_{out}}E_0$
Air cavity in εᵣ: $\frac{3\epsilon_r}{1+2\epsilon_r}E_0$ (stronger than E₀)
Cylinder ⊥: $\frac{2\epsilon_{out}}{\epsilon_{in}+\epsilon_{out}}E_0$; dielectric cyl: $\frac{2E_0}{\epsilon_r+1}$
3.4 Coated conductor in uniform E₀HW 4.24
Shell: $V=A(r-\frac{a^3}{r^2})\cos\theta$
$A=\frac{-3E_0}{(\epsilon_r+2)+2(\epsilon_r-1)a^3/b^3}$
$\mathbf E=-A[(1+\frac{2a^3}{r^3})\cos\theta\hat r-(1-\frac{a^3}{r^3})\sin\theta\hat\theta]$
3.5 Spherical shell with V(θ) or σ(θ)
Given $V=\sum c_lP_l$: in $c_l(\frac rR)^l$, out $c_l(\frac Rr)^{l+1}$
$\sigma=\frac{\epsilon_0}{R}\sum(2l+1)c_lP_l$
Given $\sigma=\sum\sigma_lP_l$: $A_l=\frac{\sigma_l}{(2l+1)\epsilon_0R^{l-1}}$, $B_l=A_lR^{2l+1}$
$\sigma=k\cos\theta$: $E_{in}=\frac{k}{3\epsilon_0}$, pointing down
3.6 Slot / box (Cartesian)
Two zero walls ⇒ $\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$; other way $e^{-n\pi x/a}$, $\sinh$, $\cosh$
Constant $V_0$ on the end: $C_n=\frac{4V_0}{n\pi}$, n odd
$V=\frac{4V_0}{\pi}\sum_{odd}\frac1ne^{-n\pi x/a}\sin\frac{n\pi y}{a}$
3.7 Wedge / pipe (polar)
Walls at 0, α ⇒ $r^{\pm k}\sin k\theta$, $k=\frac{n\pi}{\alpha}$
Zero arc at a: $(\frac ra)^k-(\frac ar)^k$; one wall at $V_0$: $V=\frac{V_0\theta}{\alpha}$
4 · IMAGES
GENERAL SETUP
Replace a conductor (or dielectric) by fictitious charges placed OUTSIDE the region you solve in
If V matches on every boundary and at ∞, uniqueness ⇒ correct there
USEFUL FACTS
Induced charge: $\sigma=-\epsilon_0\partial V/\partial n$; total induced = sum of images
Force on q: from the images only
Energy to bring q in: ½ × q × (V of images)
SPECIFIC RESULTS
4.1 Charge above a grounded plane
Image −q at the mirror point
$F=\frac{kq^2}{4d^2}$ (pull); $\sigma=-\frac{qd}{2\pi(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$; $W=-\frac{kq^2}{4d}$
Plane held at $V_0$: add $V_0$
4.2 Charge near a grounded sphere
$q'=-\frac{qR}{a}$ at $\frac{R^2}{a}$; $F=\frac{kq^2Ra}{(a^2-R^2)^2}$
Neutral sphere: add $+\frac{qR}{a}$ at center
Sphere at $V_0$: add $4\pi\epsilon_0RV_0$ at center
q inside a grounded shell at d: image $-\frac{qR}{d}$ at $\frac{R^2}{d}$
4.3 Charge in a 90° corner
Images: $-q$ at $(-x_0,y_0)$, $-q$ at $(x_0,-y_0)$, $+q$ at $(-x_0,-y_0)$
4.4 Charge above a dielectric half-space
Above: image $q'=-\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+1}q$ at −d
Below: $q''=\frac{2q}{\epsilon_r+1}$ at +d
BCs: $q+q'=q''$ and $q-q'=\epsilon_rq''$
$F=\frac{kqq'}{(2d)^2}$; $\sigma_b=-\frac{1}{2\pi}\frac{\chi_e}{\chi_e+2}\frac{qd}{(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$
4.5 Dipole above a grounded planeHW 4.6
Image: component ∥ plane flips, ⊥ component kept
$N=\frac{kp^2\sin\theta\cos\theta}{(2z)^3}$; rests ⊥ to plane
4.6 Line charge above a plane
Image $-\lambda$ (dielectric: $\times\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+1}$)
$V=\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s_-}{s_+}$; force/length $\frac{\lambda^2}{4\pi\epsilon_0d}$
5 · MULTIPOLES & DIPOLES
GENERAL SETUP
$V=k\sum_n\frac{1}{r^{n+1}}\int r'^nP_n(\cos\alpha)\rho\,d\tau'$
$=k[\frac Qr+\frac{\mathbf p\cdot\hat r}{r^2}+\frac{1}{r^3}\sum q_ir_i^2P_2(\cos\alpha_i)+\dots]$
$Q=\int\rho\,d\tau$; $\mathbf p=\int\mathbf r'\rho\,d\tau'=\sum q_i\mathbf r_i$
USEFUL FACTS
Far away the first nonzero moment wins: $V\propto r^{-(n+1)}$, $E\propto r^{-(n+2)}$
Shift origin by $\mathbf a$: $\mathbf p'=\mathbf p-Q\mathbf a$ (Q = 0 ⇒ p independent of origin)
Dipole in a field: $\mathbf N=\mathbf p\times\mathbf E$, $U=-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E$, $\mathbf F=\nabla(\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E)$
Uniform E: F = 0; aligned dipole is pulled toward stronger E
SPECIFIC RESULTS
5.1 Field of a dipole
$V=\frac{kp\cos\theta}{r^2}$
$\mathbf E=\frac{kp}{r^3}(2\cos\theta\hat r+\sin\theta\hat\theta)=\frac{k}{r^3}[3(\mathbf p\cdot\hat r)\hat r-\mathbf p]$
On axis $\frac{2kp}{r^3}$; on equator $-\frac{kp}{r^3}$
5.2 Forces between dipoles and chargesHW 4.7
Coaxial dipoles: $F=\frac{3p_1p_2}{2\pi\epsilon_0r^4}$ (attract)
$U_{12}=\frac{k}{r^3}[\mathbf p_1\cdot\mathbf p_2-3(\mathbf p_1\cdot\hat r)(\mathbf p_2\cdot\hat r)]$
Aligned dipole near charge q: $F=\frac{2kqp}{r^3}$
Sphere near q (r ≫ R): $\mathbf p=4\pi\epsilon_0R^3\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}\mathbf E$, $F\propto\frac{1}{r^5}$
5.3 Dipole moments to know
±q separated by d: $p=qd$ (from − to +)
Hemisphere shell σ: $\pi\sigma R^3$; solid hemisphere ρ: $\frac{\pi\rho R^4}{4}$
Shell $k\cos\theta$: $\frac43\pi kR^3$; ball $k\cos\theta$: $\frac{\pi kR^4}{3}$
Ring $\lambda_0\cos\phi$: $\pi R^2\lambda_0$; rod $\lambda_0z/L$ on [−L, L]: $\frac23\lambda_0L^2$
5.4 Linear quadrupole (q, −2q, q)
Q = 0, p = 0: $V=\frac{kqa^2(3\cos^2\theta-1)}{r^3}$
Force in nonuniform E: add $q_i\mathbf E(\mathbf r_i)$
5.5 Overlapping balls ±ρ
Overlap: $\mathbf E=-\frac{\rho\mathbf d}{3\epsilon_0}$ uniform (d from − center to + center)
Cylinders: $-\frac{\rho\mathbf d}{2\epsilon_0}$; d → 0, ρd = P: polarized sphere
6 · EXAM I BASICS: COULOMB, GAUSS, CONDUCTORS
GENERAL SETUP
$\mathbf E=k\int\frac{dq\,\widehat{\mathbf r-\mathbf r'}}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|^2}$, $V=k\int\frac{dq}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}$, $dq=\lambda dl,\ \sigma da,\ \rho d\tau$
$\oint\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf a=\frac{Q_{enc}}{\epsilon_0}$; $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$; $\nabla^2V=-\frac{\rho}{\epsilon_0}$
$V(b)-V(a)=-\int_a^b\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$; $W=q\Delta V$
USEFUL FACTS
Conductor: E = 0 inside, V constant, charge on surface, $E=\sigma/\epsilon_0$ just outside
Charge q in a cavity ⇒ −q on the cavity wall
$E^\perp$ jumps by $\sigma/\epsilon_0$; $E^\parallel$ and V continuous
Given E: $\rho=\epsilon_0\nabla\cdot\mathbf E$; $Q_{enc}(r)=4\pi\epsilon_0r^2E_r$; ≠ 0 as r → 0 ⇒ point charge
$\nabla\cdot\frac{\hat r}{r^2}=4\pi\delta^3(\mathbf r)$; $\nabla\cdot\frac{\hat s}{s}=2\pi\delta^2$
SPECIFIC RESULTS
6.1 Point charge / ball
Outside: $\frac{kQ}{r^2}$; inside uniform ball: $\frac{\rho r}{3\epsilon_0}$
$V_{in}=\frac{kQ}{2R}(3-\frac{r^2}{R^2})$; $\rho=kr^n$: $E=\frac{kr^{n+1}}{(n+3)\epsilon_0}$
6.2 Line / cylinder
Line: $\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0s}$, $V=-\frac{\lambda}{2\pi\epsilon_0}\ln\frac{s}{s_0}$
Inside uniform cylinder: $\frac{\rho s}{2\epsilon_0}$; $\rho=ks^n$: $\frac{ks^{n+1}}{(n+2)\epsilon_0}$
6.3 Sheet / slab
Sheet: $\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}$ each side; conductor surface $\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_0}$
Slab |z|<a: $\frac{\rho z}{\epsilon_0}$ inside, $\frac{\rho a}{\epsilon_0}$ outside (constant)
6.4 Ring / disk / segment (axis)
Ring: $E=\frac{kQz}{(z^2+R^2)^{3/2}}$, $V=\frac{kQ}{\sqrt{z^2+R^2}}$
Disk: $E=\frac{\sigma}{2\epsilon_0}(1-\frac{z}{\sqrt{z^2+R^2}})$
Segment: $E_\perp=\frac{k\lambda}{s}(\sin\theta_2-\sin\theta_1)$
6.5 Overlaps and cavities
Hole = whole shape + negative copy; each field from its own center
Cavity offset d in ball ρ: $\mathbf E=\frac{\rho\mathbf d}{3\epsilon_0}$ uniform
7 · MATH
Div/curl one-liners
$F(r)\hat r$: div $\frac{1}{r^2}(r^2F)'$
$F(s)\hat s$: div $\frac1s(sF)'$
$F(s)\hat\phi$: curl $\frac1s(sF)'\hat z$
$F(s)\hat z$: curl $-F'\hat\phi$
$F(z)\hat x$: curl $F'\hat y$
Cylindrical
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=\frac1s\partial_s(sA_s)+\frac1s\partial_\phi A_\phi+\partial_zA_z$
$\nabla\times\mathbf A=[\frac1s\partial_\phi A_z-\partial_zA_\phi]\hat s+[\partial_zA_s-\partial_sA_z]\hat\phi+\frac1s[\partial_s(sA_\phi)-\partial_\phi A_s]\hat z$
$\nabla^2f=\frac1s\partial_s(s\partial_sf)+\frac{1}{s^2}\partial_\phi^2f+\partial_z^2f$
Spherical
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=\frac{1}{r^2}\partial_r(r^2A_r)+\frac{\partial_\theta(\sin\theta A_\theta)}{r\sin\theta}+\frac{\partial_\phi A_\phi}{r\sin\theta}$

8 · CURRENTS, FORCES, BIOT–SAVART
GENERAL SETUP
$I=\lambda v$ (A); $\mathbf K=\sigma\mathbf v$ (A/m); $\mathbf J=\rho\mathbf v$ (A/m²)
$\mathbf F=q(\mathbf E+\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)$; on a wire $\mathbf F=\int I\,d\mathbf l\times\mathbf B$
$\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{I\,d\mathbf l\times\widehat{\mathbf r-\mathbf r'}}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|^2}$ (or $\mathbf K\,da$, $\mathbf J\,d\tau$)
Steady currents: $\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0$
USEFUL FACTS
RHR: thumb along I, fingers curl with B
Pieces: straight piece whose LINE passes through P gives 0
Arc of angle φ, radius R, at center: $\frac{\mu_0I\phi}{4\pi R}$
Semi-infinite wire ending beside P (distance s): $\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi s}$
Spinning: disk $K=\sigma\omega s$; shell $K=\sigma\omega R\sin\theta$; ball $J=\rho\omega r\sin\theta$; ring $I=\frac{Q\omega}{2\pi}$
Uniform B: closed loop F = 0, torque $\mathbf m\times\mathbf B$; curved wire = straight chord
Cyclotron: $r=\frac{mv}{qB}$, $\omega=\frac{qB}{m}$; B does no work

9 · AMPÈRE & SUPERPOSITION
GENERAL SETUP
$\oint\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf l=\mu_0I_{enc}$
$\nabla\times\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf J$; $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B=0$
USEFUL FACTS
Works only for line, plane, solenoid, toroid symmetry (or sums)
Symmetry: $B_s=0$ (∇·B = 0), $B_z=0$ (rectangle to ∞) ⇒ $\mathbf B=B(s)\hat\phi$
Loops: circle (wire), rectangle straddling (sheet), rectangle half inside (solenoid)
$I_{enc}=\int J(s)\,2\pi s\,ds$
Inside a uniform wire: $\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}{2}\mathbf J\times\mathbf s$ (s from that wire's axis)
Holes/overlaps: superpose full shapes with +J and −J
SPECIFIC RESULTS
9.1 Solid wire, uniform J
Inside $\frac{\mu_0Is}{2\pi a^2}$; outside $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}$
Current only on surface: 0 inside
9.2 Wire with J ∝ sⁿ; thick tubeHW 5.14
$I_{enc}=I(\frac sa)^{n+2}$, $B=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}(\frac sa)^{n+2}$
$J=ks$: $B_{in}=\frac{\mu_0Is^2}{2\pi a^3}$; $J=k/s$: $B=\mu_0k$
Tube a..b: $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}\frac{s^2-a^2}{b^2-a^2}$
9.3 Coax
Between: $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}$; outside: 0

$\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}{2}\mathbf K\times\hat n$ ($\hat n$ from sheet toward point)

9.5 Moving capacitor (two opposite sheets)HW 5.18

9.8 Cylinder with an off-axis hole
Wire radius a, hole radius c, hole axis at $\mathbf b=b\hat x$, total I (+z)
$J=\frac{I}{\pi(a^2-c^2)}$; $I_+=\frac{Ia^2}{a^2-c^2}$, $I_-=-\frac{Ic^2}{a^2-c^2}$

Why: full wire (+J) + hole wire (−J); positions cancel.

10 · VECTOR POTENTIAL A
GENERAL SETUP
$\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A$; Coulomb gauge $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=0$
$\nabla^2\mathbf A=-\mu_0\mathbf J$; $\mathbf A=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\int\frac{\mathbf J}{|\mathbf r-\mathbf r'|}d\tau'$ (localized currents only)
$\oint\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf l=\Phi_B$
USEFUL FACTS
A points along J
$A_z(s)\hat z$: $B_\phi=-\frac{dA_z}{ds}$
$A_\phi(s)\hat\phi$: $B_z=\frac1s\frac{d}{ds}(sA_\phi)$, or $A_\phi\cdot2\pi s=\Phi$
$A_x(z)\hat x$: $B_y=\frac{dA_x}{dz}$
$A_\phi(r,\theta)$: $B_r=\frac{\partial_\theta(\sin\theta A_\phi)}{r\sin\theta}$, $B_\theta=-\frac{\partial_r(rA_\phi)}{r}$
A continuous; $\partial_n\mathbf A$ jumps by $-\mu_0\mathbf K$; finite on axis
Infinite currents: the integral diverges ⇒ solve the curl ODE instead

12 · B, H, M: MAGNETIC MATTER
GENERAL SETUP
$\mathbf M$ = magnetic moment per volume (A/m)
Bound current: $\mathbf J_b=\nabla\times\mathbf M$, $\mathbf K_b=\mathbf M\times\hat n$
$\mathbf H\equiv\frac{\mathbf B}{\mu_0}-\mathbf M$: $\nabla\times\mathbf H=\mathbf J_f$, $\oint\mathbf H\cdot d\mathbf l=I_{f,enc}$
$\nabla\cdot\mathbf H=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf M$ (NOT zero in general)
USEFUL FACTS
Linear: $\mathbf M=\chi_m\mathbf H$ and $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$
$\mu=\mu_0(1+\chi_m)=\mu_0\mu_r$, so $\mu_r=1+\chi_m$
Homogeneous: $\mathbf J_b=\chi_m\mathbf J_f$
Para: $\chi_m>0$ (~10⁻⁵), pulled in; dia: $\chi_m<0$, pushed out; ferro: keeps M
No free current + symmetry ⇒ H = 0 ⇒ B = μ₀M
Free current: H from Ampère, then $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$ (or $\mu_0(\mathbf H+\mathbf M)$)
Shape: $\mathbf B_{in}=\mu_0(1-N)\mathbf M$, $\mathbf H_{in}=-N\mathbf M$
P charges the caps; M puts current on the sides
SPECIFIC RESULTS
12.1 Uniformly magnetized sphere
$\mathbf K_b=M\sin\theta\hat\phi$ (= spinning shell with σωR → M)
$\mathbf B_{in}=\frac23\mu_0\mathbf M$, $\mathbf H_{in}=-\frac{\mathbf M}{3}$
Outside: dipole $\frac43\pi R^3\mathbf M$; uniform-M shell: B = 0 in cavity
12.2 Magnetized rod / disk
Long rod ∥ M: $B=\mu_0M$ (solenoid with nI → M)
Thin disk ⊥: B ≈ 0, H ≈ −M
Finite rod on axis: $\frac{\mu_0M}{2}(\cos\theta_2-\cos\theta_1)$
12.3 Cylinder with M along φ̂HW 6.8
$M=ks^2\hat\phi$: $\mathbf J_b=3ks\hat z$, $\mathbf K_b=-kR^2\hat z$
$\mathbf B=\mu_0\mathbf M$ inside, 0 outside (H = 0)
12.4 Cylinder with M = k s ẑHW 6.12

13 · MAGNETIC BOUNDARY CONDITIONS & E ↔ B
GENERAL SETUP
$B^\perp$ continuous; $\mathbf B_{above}-\mathbf B_{below}=\mu_0\mathbf K\times\hat n$
$\mathbf H^\parallel$ jumps by $\mathbf K_f\times\hat n$
$\mu_1H_1^\perp=\mu_2H_2^\perp$ (no free surface current)
A continuous; $\partial_n\mathbf A$ jumps by $-\mu_0\mathbf K$
USEFUL FACTS
ρ ↔ J; ε₀ ↔ 1/μ₀; V ↔ A (per component)
Gauss ↔ Ampère; $\frac{p}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ ↔ $\frac{\mu_0m}{4\pi}$
D ↔ H; P ↔ M
Sphere: −P/3ε₀ ↔ +⅔μ₀M; long rod: ≈0 ↔ μ₀M
Fill all space: E ÷ εᵣ ↔ B × μᵣ
