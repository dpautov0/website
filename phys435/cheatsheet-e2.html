PHYS 435 · Exam II · FRONT: P, D, E (dielectrics)$k=\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_0}$ · $\epsilon=\epsilon_0\epsilon_r=\epsilon_0(1+\chi_e)$

1 · The D → E → P chain (do it in this order)

  • D from Gauss on free charge only: $\oint \mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf a=Q_{f,enc}$. Needs symmetry (sphere/cyl/plane).
  • E $=\mathbf D/\epsilon$ (linear) or $(\mathbf D-\mathbf P)/\epsilon_0$ (frozen P).
  • P $=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E=\mathbf D-\epsilon_0\mathbf E=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\mathbf D=(1-\frac1{\epsilon_r})\mathbf D$.
  • Bound: $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\hat n$ ($\hat n$ OUT of the dielectric; inner surface $\hat n=-\hat r$). $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$.
  • Linear, homogeneous: $\rho_b=-\frac{\chi_e}{1+\chi_e}\rho_f$ → no free inside ⇒ no $\rho_b$.
  • V $=-\int\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$ region by region from ∞ inward; keep V continuous.
  • Check: total bound charge $=0$. Next to a conductor: $\sigma_f+\sigma_b=\sigma_f/\epsilon_r$.

2 · Shortcuts that skip the chain

  • No free charge + symmetry ⇒ D = 0 ⇒ E = −P/ε₀ inside, 0 outside.
  • Trap: $\nabla\times\mathbf D=\nabla\times\mathbf P\neq0$; no symmetry ⇒ D NOT fixed by $\rho_f$ (bar electret: $\rho_f=0$, $\mathbf D\ne0$).
  • Free charge only on the surface of a dielectric sphere ⇒ inside D=E=P=0; dielectric does nothing.
  • Fill all space with $\epsilon$: every field from free charge ÷ $\epsilon_r$.

3 · Divergence one-liners

$F(r)\hat r$: $\frac1{r^2}(r^2F)'$ · $F(s)\hat s$: $\frac1s(sF)'$ · $F(z)\hat z$: $F'$. Curl of these = 0.

$P=kr^n\hat r$: $\rho_b=-(n+2)kr^{n-1}$. $P=ks^n\hat s$: $\rho_b=-(n+1)ks^{n-1}$. $\sigma_b=P(R)$.

$\hat r/r^2$ hides $4\pi\delta^3$; $\hat s/s$ hides $2\pi\delta^2$ (line charge).

4 · Uniform P (frozen)

  • $\rho_b=0$; $\sigma_b=\pm P$ only on faces P pierces (sphere: $P\cos\theta$).
  • $\mathbf E_{in}=-N\mathbf P/\epsilon_0$: slab⊥ $N{=}1$, sphere $\frac13$, cyl⊥ $\frac12$, needle ∥ 0.
  • Sphere outside = pure dipole $\mathbf p=\frac43\pi R^3\mathbf P$: $V=\frac{PR^3\cos\theta}{3\epsilon_0r^2}$.
  • Shell $a{<}r{<}b$ uniform P: E = 0 in cavity (ball − ball).
  • Offset balls $\pm\rho$: overlap $\mathbf E=-\rho\mathbf d/3\epsilon_0$ ($\mathbf d$: − to + center). $\rho\mathbf d\to\mathbf P$.
  • Cavity in polarized slab (P⊥): $-\frac{2P}{3\epsilon_0}$; outside: dipole $-\frac43\pi R^3P$.

5 · Must-know P problems

  • Sphere $\mathbf P=k\mathbf r$: $\sigma_b=kR$, $\rho_b=-3k$, $\mathbf E_{in}=-k\mathbf r/\epsilon_0$, out 0.
  • Shell $\mathbf P=\frac kr\hat r$: $\rho_b=-\frac k{r^2}$, $\sigma_b(a)=-\frac ka$, $\sigma_b(b)=\frac kb$, $E=-\frac{k}{\epsilon_0r}$ in shell.
  • Cyl $\mathbf P=ks\hat s$: $\rho_b=-2k$, $\sigma_b=ka$, $E=-ks/\epsilon_0$.
  • Bar electret (4.11): caps $\pm P$; long → two point charges; short → capacitor $E\approx -P/\epsilon_0$.

6 · Linear: standard free-charge setups

  • Point q in $\epsilon$: $E=\frac{q}{4\pi\epsilon r^2}$; bound at q: $-q\frac{\chi_e}{1+\chi_e}$.
  • Conductor Q, radius a, shell to b: $D=\frac{Q}{4\pi r^2}$; $V(a)=kQ[\frac1b+\frac1{\epsilon_r}(\frac1a-\frac1b)]$; $\sigma_b(a)=-\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{Q}{4\pi a^2}$, $\sigma_b(b)=+\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{Q}{4\pi b^2}$.
  • Ball of $\epsilon_r$, uniform $\rho_f$: $D=\rho_fr/3$, $\sigma_b=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\frac{\rho_fR}3$.
  • Nonuniform $\epsilon(x)$: D still $=\sigma$; $\rho_b=-dP/dx\ne0$.

7 · Capacitors = springs

  • Stacked (series): same $D=\sigma_f$; $E_i=\sigma/\epsilon_i$; $\frac1C=\sum\frac{d_i}{\epsilon_iA}$.
  • Side by side (parallel): same $E=V/d$; $D_i=\epsilon_iE$; $C=\sum\frac{\epsilon_iA_i}d$.
  • Half fill: stacked $\frac{2\epsilon_r}{1+\epsilon_r}C_0$, side $\frac{1+\epsilon_r}2C_0$.
  • Coax: $\frac CL=\frac{2\pi\epsilon_0}{\sum\ln(b_i/a_i)/\epsilon_{ri}}$. Sphere: $\frac1C=\sum\frac{1}{4\pi\epsilon_i}(\frac1{a_i}-\frac1{b_i})$.
  • HW 4.18 ($\epsilon_r$ 2, 1.5): $P=\sigma/2,\sigma/3$; $V=\frac{7\sigma a}{6\epsilon_0}$.

8 · Boundary conditions

  • $D^\perp_1-D^\perp_2=\sigma_f$; $E^\parallel$ continuous; V continuous.
  • No free: $\epsilon_1\partial_nV_1=\epsilon_2\partial_nV_2$. $\frac{\tan\theta_1}{\tan\theta_2}=\frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}$ (bends away from normal into high ε).

9 · Sphere / cylinder in uniform $E_0$

$V_{in}=Ar\cos\theta$, $V_{out}=-E_0r\cos\theta+\frac{B\cos\theta}{r^2}$. BCs at R: V cont.; $\epsilon_rA=-E_0-\frac{2B}{R^3}$.

$A=-\frac{3E_0}{\epsilon_r+2}$, $B=\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0R^3$; $P=3\epsilon_0\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0$; $\sigma_b=P\cos\theta$; $E_r^{out}(0)=\frac{3\epsilon_r}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0$.

General: sphere $E_{in}=\frac{3\epsilon_{out}}{\epsilon_{in}+2\epsilon_{out}}E_0$; cyl⊥ $\frac{2\epsilon_{out}}{\epsilon_{in}+\epsilon_{out}}E_0$. Cavity: $\frac{3\epsilon_r}{1+2\epsilon_r}$. Conductor: $\epsilon_{in}\to\infty$.

Coated conductor a, shell to b (4.24): $V=A(r-\frac{a^3}{r^2})\cos\theta$, $A=\frac{-3E_0}{(\epsilon_r+2)+2(\epsilon_r-1)a^3/b^3}$.

Check: $\epsilon_r\to1$ vacuum; $\epsilon_r\to\infty$ conductor.

10 · Images with a dielectric

q at height d above $\epsilon_r$: above use $q'=-\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+1}q$ at $-d$; below use $q''=\frac{2q}{\epsilon_r+1}$ at $+d$ (with $k$).

$F=\frac{kqq'}{(2d)^2}$; $\sigma_b=-\frac1{2\pi}\frac{\chi_e}{\chi_e+2}\frac{qd}{(s^2+d^2)^{3/2}}$; $W=\frac12\cdot\frac{kqq'}{2d}$. Line charge: same fraction.

Plane at $V_0$: add $V_0$. Charge Q on sphere in $E_0$: add $\frac{Q}{4\pi\epsilon r}$.

11 · Energy

  • $W=\frac12\int\mathbf D\cdot\mathbf E\,d\tau=\frac12\int\frac{D^2}{\epsilon}d\tau$ (free work). $\frac{\epsilon_0}2\int E^2$ = all charge incl. bound. Frozen P, no free: $\frac12\int D\cdot E=0$!
  • Fastest: $\frac12QV$, $\frac{Q^2}{2C}$. Outside a sphere: always $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}$.
  • Ball: $\frac{3Q^2}{20\pi\epsilon_0R}$; ball of $\epsilon_r$: $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}(1+\frac1{5\epsilon_r})$; 4.26: $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0\epsilon_r}(\frac1a+\frac{\chi_e}b)$.
  • Fixed Q: W ÷ $\epsilon_r$. Fixed V: W × $\epsilon_r$. Force $F=\frac12V^2\frac{dC}{dx}$ (toward higher C). Slab: $\frac{\epsilon_0\chi_ewV^2}{2d}$.
  • Infinite line/cyl: energy/length diverges outside ($\ln$).

12 · Dipoles & multipoles

  • $Q=\sum q$, $\mathbf p=\sum q_i\mathbf r_i=\int\mathbf r\rho$; $Q{=}0$ ⇒ p origin-independent; shift: $\mathbf p'=\mathbf p-Q\mathbf a$.
  • $V=k[\frac Qr+\frac{\mathbf p\cdot\hat r}{r^2}+\frac1{r^3}\sum q_ir_i^2P_2(\cos\alpha_i)]$; $P_2=\frac{3x^2-1}2$.
  • $\mathbf E_{dip}=\frac{kp}{r^3}(2\cos\theta\hat r+\sin\theta\hat\theta)$.
  • $\mathbf N=\mathbf p\times\mathbf E$, $U=-\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E$, $\mathbf F=\nabla(\mathbf p\cdot\mathbf E)$; uniform E: F = 0.
  • Image of p in plane: ∥ part flips, ⊥ part kept. Rests ⊥ plane. Coaxial dipoles: $F=\frac{3p_1p_2}{2\pi\epsilon_0r^4}$, attract.

14 · Off-axis hole (Ampère + superposition)

  • Wire radius a, hole radius b, offset $\mathbf d$, total current I ⇒ $J=\frac{I}{\pi(a^2-b^2)}$.
  • Full wire: $I_+=J\pi a^2=I\frac{a^2}{a^2-b^2}$. Hole wire: $I_-=-J\pi b^2=-I\frac{b^2}{a^2-b^2}$. ($I_++I_-=I$ ✓)
  • Inside a uniform wire: $\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}2\mathbf J\times\mathbf s$ (s from ITS axis). Outside: $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}\hat\phi$.
  • In hole: $\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}2\mathbf J\times\mathbf d$, uniform, $|B|=\frac{\mu_0Id}{2\pi(a^2-b^2)}$, ⊥ to d.
  • In metal: $\frac{\mu_0}2\mathbf J\times\mathbf s-\frac{\mu_0|I_-|}{2\pi s'}\hat\phi'$ ($s'$ from hole axis).
  • Outside: $\frac{\mu_0I_+}{2\pi s}\hat\phi+\frac{\mu_0I_-}{2\pi s'}\hat\phi'$. On the line through both axes: add with signs.
  • Opposite-J overlapping wires: $\mathbf B=\frac{\mu_0}2\mathbf J\times(\mathbf c_--\mathbf c_+)$ in overlap.
  • A in hole: $A_z=-\frac{\mu_0J}4(s^2-|\mathbf s-\mathbf d|^2)$ (linear in s ⇒ uniform B).

15 · Vector potential WITHOUT integrating

  • $\mathbf B=\nabla\times\mathbf A$, $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A=0$, $\nabla^2\mathbf A=-\mu_0\mathbf J$. A ∥ J (A follows the current).
  • $\mathbf B=B(s)\hat\phi$ ⇒ $\mathbf A=A_z(s)\hat z$, $B_\phi=-\frac{dA_z}{ds}$.
  • $\mathbf B=B(s)\hat z$ ⇒ $\mathbf A=A_\phi(s)\hat\phi$, $B_z=\frac1s\frac{d}{ds}(sA_\phi)$; or Stokes: $A_\phi2\pi s=\Phi(s)$.
  • $\mathbf B=B(z)\hat y$ ⇒ $A_x(z)$, $B_y=\partial_zA_x$.
  • Spherical $\mathbf A=A_\phi(r,\theta)\hat\phi$: $B_r=\frac1{r\sin\theta}\partial_\theta(\sin\theta A_\phi)$, $B_\theta=-\frac1r\partial_r(rA_\phi)$.
  • Then $\mu_0\mathbf J=\nabla\times\mathbf B$: $\hat\phi$ field: $\frac1s(sB_\phi)'\hat z$; $\hat z$ field: $-B_z'\hat\phi$.
  • A continuous across K; $\partial_n\mathbf A$ jumps by $-\mu_0\mathbf K$. Integral to ∞ diverges ⇒ use curl/Stokes.

16 · Ampère: the 4 symmetric shapes

  • Wire (uniform): $\frac{\mu_0Is}{2\pi a^2}$ in, $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}$ out. $J\propto s^k$: $B_{in}=\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}(\frac sa)^{k+2}$.
  • Sheet: $\frac{\mu_0K}2$, dir $\mathbf K\times\hat n$. Two opposite sheets: $\mu_0K$ between, 0 out.
  • Slab $|z|<a$, $J\hat x$: $-\mu_0Jz\hat y$ in, $\mp\mu_0Ja\hat y$ out.
  • Solenoid $\mu_0nI$ (0 out); toroid $\frac{\mu_0NI}{2\pi s}$; coax $\frac{\mu_0I}{2\pi s}$ between, 0 out.
  • Coaxial solenoids opposite: $\mu_0(n_1-n_2)I$, $\mu_0n_2I$, 0. Moving capacitor: $\mu_0\sigma v$; balance at $v=c$.
  • Force on a sheet: use OTHER sheet's field (avg).

17 · Biot–Savart pieces

  • Segment: $\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi s}(\sin\theta_2-\sin\theta_1)$ (θ from ⊥ foot). On its line: 0.
  • Semi-∞ beside end: $\frac{\mu_0I}{4\pi s}$. Arc φ at center: $\frac{\mu_0I\phi}{4\pi R}$.
  • Loop axis: $\frac{\mu_0IR^2}{2(R^2+z^2)^{3/2}}$. n-gon: $\frac{n\mu_0I}{2\pi R}\sin\frac\pi n$; square $\frac{\sqrt2\mu_0I}{\pi R}$.
  • Finite solenoid axis $\frac{\mu_0nI}2(\cos\theta_2-\cos\theta_1)$; mouth $\frac{\mu_0nI}2$. Helmholtz d=R: $\frac{8\mu_0I}{5\sqrt5R}$.
  • $\mathbf F=I\!\int d\mathbf l\times\mathbf B$; uniform B: closed loop F=0, curved wire = chord. Wires: $\frac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$, same dir attract.
  • Loop near wire: $\frac{\mu_0I^2a^2}{2\pi s(s+a)}$. $\mathbf B=kz\hat x$ loop: $Ika^2\hat z$.

18 · Spinning charge & dipole m

  • $K=\sigma\omega s$ (disk), $\sigma\omega R\sin\theta$ (shell); $J=\rho\omega r\sin\theta$ (ball). sinθ!
  • $m=\frac{Q}{2M}L$: ring $\frac12Q\omega R^2$, disk $\frac14$, shell $\frac13$, ball $\frac15$.
  • Center B ($\frac{\mu_0Q\omega}{\pi R}$): ring ¼, disk ½, shell ⅙, ball ¼. Shell inside uniform $\frac23\mu_0\sigma\omega R$.
  • $\mathbf m=I\mathbf a$; $\mathbf B_{dip}=\frac{\mu_0m}{4\pi r^3}(2\cos\theta\hat r+\sin\theta\hat\theta)$; no monopole term.
  • $\mathbf N=\mathbf m\times\mathbf B$, $U=-\mathbf m\cdot\mathbf B$, $\mathbf F=\nabla(\mathbf m\cdot\mathbf B)$; coaxial $\frac{3\mu_0m_1m_2}{2\pi r^4}$.
  • $B_{avg}$ in sphere $=\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2\mathbf m}{R^3}$; solid spin ball: $\frac{\mu_0Q\omega}{10\pi R}$.

19 · Magnetic BCs

$B^\perp$ continuous; $\mathbf B_{a}-\mathbf B_{b}=\mu_0\mathbf K\times\hat n$; $\mathbf H^\parallel_a-\mathbf H^\parallel_b=\mathbf K_f\times\hat n$; $\mu_1H_1^\perp=\mu_2H_2^\perp$. Inside from outside: one BC at the equator.

20 · M, bound currents, H

  • $\mathbf J_b=\nabla\times\mathbf M$, $\mathbf K_b=\mathbf M\times\hat n$ (faces ∥ M). $\mathbf H=\frac{\mathbf B}{\mu_0}-\mathbf M$, $\oint\mathbf H\cdot d\mathbf l=I_f$.
  • No free I + symmetry ⇒ H = 0 ⇒ B = μ₀M. Free I: H from Ampère, $\mathbf B=\mu_0(\mathbf H+\mathbf M)$.
  • Linear: $\mathbf M=\chi_m\mathbf H$, $\mathbf B=\mu\mathbf H$, $\mathbf J_b=\chi_m\mathbf J_f$. Coax $\chi_m$: $I_b=\chi_mI$ on inner surface.
  • Uniform M: sphere $\frac23\mu_0\mathbf M$ in, $\mathbf m=\frac43\pi R^3\mathbf M$; long rod $\mu_0\mathbf M$; thin disk ≈0. $\mathbf B_{in}=\mu_0(1-N)\mathbf M$, $\mathbf H_{in}=-N\mathbf M$.
  • $\mathbf M=ks^2\hat\phi$: $J_b=3ks\hat z$, $K_b=-kR^2\hat z$, B=μ₀M. $\mathbf M=ks\hat z$: $J_b=-k\hat\phi$, $K_b=kR\hat\phi$, B=μ₀M.
  • Para: M ∥ B, $\chi_m{>}0$, pulled in. Dia: M anti-∥, $\chi_m{<}0$, pushed out. Ferro: keeps M.
  • Half-cyl coax $\mu_1|\mu_2$: B shared: $B=\frac{\mu_1\mu_2}{\mu_1+\mu_2}\frac{I}{\pi s}$.
