1 · E, D, P: FIELDS IN DIELECTRICS
GENERAL SETUP
$\mathbf E$ = total field from ALL charge: $\nabla\cdot\mathbf E=\frac{\rho_f+\rho_b}{\epsilon_0}$, $\nabla\times\mathbf E=0$, $\mathbf E=-\nabla V$
$\mathbf P$ = dipole moment per volume (C/m²)
Bound charge: $\sigma_b=\mathbf P\cdot\hat n$ ($\hat n$ out of the material), $\rho_b=-\nabla\cdot\mathbf P$
$\mathbf D\equiv\epsilon_0\mathbf E+\mathbf P$ (C/m²): $\nabla\cdot\mathbf D=\rho_f$, $\oint\mathbf D\cdot d\mathbf a=Q_{f,enc}$
$\nabla\times\mathbf D=\nabla\times\mathbf P$ (NOT zero in general)
USEFUL FACTS
Linear: $\mathbf P=\epsilon_0\chi_e\mathbf E$ and $\mathbf D=\epsilon\mathbf E$
$\epsilon=\epsilon_0(1+\chi_e)=\epsilon_0\epsilon_r$; $\epsilon_r=1+\chi_e$; $\chi_e=\epsilon_r-1$
Vacuum: $\chi_e=0$, $\epsilon_r=1$, $\epsilon=\epsilon_0$
$\mathbf P=\mathbf D-\epsilon_0\mathbf E=\frac{\chi_e}{\epsilon_r}\mathbf D=(1-\frac{1}{\epsilon_r})\mathbf D$
$\mathbf E=\mathbf D/\epsilon$ (linear); $\mathbf E=(\mathbf D-\mathbf P)/\epsilon_0$ (frozen P: no ε!)
Homogeneous linear: $\rho_b=-\frac{\chi_e}{1+\chi_e}\rho_f$, so no free charge inside ⇒ no $\rho_b$
Next to a conductor: $\sigma_f+\sigma_b=\sigma_f/\epsilon_r$
No free charge + symmetry ⇒ $\mathbf D=0$ ⇒ $\mathbf E=-\mathbf P/\epsilon_0$ inside, 0 outside
Total bound charge is always 0 (use it as a check)
Recipe: D (Gauss, free charge) → E = D/ε → P → $\sigma_b$, $\rho_b$ → V from ∞ inward

2 · CAPACITORS & ENERGY
GENERAL SETUP
$C=Q/\Delta V$; $W=\frac12QV=\frac12CV^2=\frac{Q^2}{2C}$
All charge: $W=\frac{\epsilon_0}{2}\int E^2d\tau=\frac12\int\rho V\,d\tau$
With dielectric (work on free charge): $W=\frac12\int\mathbf D\cdot\mathbf E\,d\tau=\frac12\int\frac{D^2}{\epsilon}d\tau$
USEFUL FACTS
Stacked layers = series (same D): $\frac1C=\sum\frac{1}{C_i}$
Side by side = parallel (same E): $C=\sum C_i$
Fill with $\epsilon_r$: fixed Q ⇒ W ÷ $\epsilon_r$; fixed V ⇒ W × $\epsilon_r$
Force: $F=\frac12V^2\frac{dC}{dx}$, toward higher C
Conductor surface pressure: $\frac{\sigma^2}{2\epsilon_0}$; energy outside a sphere: $\frac{Q^2}{8\pi\epsilon_0R}$
Infinite line/cylinder: energy per length diverges outside
SPECIFIC RESULTS (setup → solve → boxed result)

Solve: $E_i=\frac{\sigma}{\epsilon_i}$; $V=\sum E_id_i$; $C=\frac{\sigma A}{V}$; $P_i=\sigma(1-\frac{1}{\epsilon_{ri}})$; interface $\sigma_b=P_1-P_2$

4.18 ($\epsilon_r$ 2 and 1.5, each thickness a): $V=\frac{7\sigma a}{6\epsilon_0}$, $\sigma_b$(interface) $=\frac{\sigma}{6}$

2.3 CoaxHW 4.21

3 · BOUNDARY CONDITIONS & LAPLACE PROBLEMS
GENERAL SETUP
No charge in a region ⇒ $\nabla^2V=0$
Sphere: $V=\sum(A_lr^l+\frac{B_l}{r^{l+1}})P_l(\cos\theta)$
Cylinder: $A_0+B_0\ln s+\sum(A_ns^n+B_ns^{-n})(C_n\cos n\phi+D_n\sin n\phi)$
Box: $e^{\pm kx}\sin ky$, $\sinh$, $\cosh$
BCs: $D_1^\perp-D_2^\perp=\sigma_f$; $\mathbf E^\parallel$ continuous; V continuous
No free σ: $\epsilon_1\partial_nV_1=\epsilon_2\partial_nV_2$
Charged sheet in vacuum: $\partial_nV_{out}-\partial_nV_{in}=-\sigma/\epsilon_0$
USEFUL FACTS
Center finite ⇒ drop $r^{-(l+1)}$, $s^{-n}$, $\ln s$
Far away ⇒ drop $r^l$, except $-E_0r\cos\theta$ for a uniform field
Uniform field ⇒ only l = 1 (n = 1) terms appear
$P_0=1$, $P_1=\cos\theta$, $P_2=\frac{3\cos^2\theta-1}{2}$, $P_3=\frac{5\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta}{2}$
$\cos^2\theta=\frac13+\frac23P_2$; $c_l=\frac{2l+1}{2}\int_{-1}^1f(x)P_l(x)\,dx$
Field lines bend away from the normal entering high ε: $\frac{\tan\theta_1}{\tan\theta_2}=\frac{\epsilon_1}{\epsilon_2}$
Uniqueness: anything that meets every BC is THE answer
Recipe: regions → general V → kill terms → number BCs → match each $P_l$ → check limits

3.1 Dielectric sphere in a uniform field E₀
Setup: $V_{in}=Ar\cos\theta$, $V_{out}=-E_0r\cos\theta+\frac{B\cos\theta}{r^2}$
BCs at R: (i) V continuous (ii) $\epsilon_r\partial_rV_{in}=\partial_rV_{out}$
Solve: (i) $AR=-E_0R+\frac{B}{R^2}$ (ii) $\epsilon_rA=-E_0-\frac{2B}{R^3}$; eliminate B
$A=-\frac{3E_0}{\epsilon_r+2}$, $E_{in}=\frac{3E_0}{\epsilon_r+2}$ uniform, $B=\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0R^3$
$P=3\epsilon_0\frac{\epsilon_r-1}{\epsilon_r+2}E_0$, $\sigma_b=P\cos\theta$
Check: $\epsilon_r=1$ ⇒ vacuum; $\epsilon_r\to\infty$ ⇒ conductor
3.2 Conducting sphere in a uniform field E₀

3.3 Bubble, or any εin inside εout

3.5 Spherical shell with V(θ) or σ(θ)

3.6 Slot / box (Cartesian)

4 · IMAGES

USEFUL FACTS

Energy to bring q in: ½ × q × (V of the images)

5 · MULTIPOLES & DIPOLES
GENERAL SETUP
$V=k\sum_n\frac{1}{r^{n+1}}\int r'^nP_n(\cos\alpha)\rho\,d\tau'$
$=k[\frac Qr+\frac{\mathbf p\cdot\hat r}{r^2}+\frac{1}{r^3}\sum q_ir_i^2P_2(\cos\alpha_i)+\dots]$
$Q=\int\rho\,d\tau$; $\mathbf p=\int\mathbf r'\rho\,d\tau'=\sum q_i\mathbf r_i$
USEFUL FACTS
