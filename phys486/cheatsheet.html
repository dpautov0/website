1 · Postulates and measurement

  • State: normalized $\ket\psi=\sum_n c_n\ket{a_n}$, $c_n=\braket{a_n|\psi}$, $\sum|c_n|^2=1$. Global phase unphysical; relative phases physical.
  • Outcomes = eigenvalues of Hermitian $\hat A$. Born: $P(a_n)=|\braket{a_n|\psi}|^2$. Degenerate: add over the eigenspace.
  • Collapse: after $a_n$ the state is $\ket{a_n}$ (degenerate: project onto the eigenspace, renormalize). Repeat at once → same result. Measuring prepares states; $N$-dim space ⇒ ≤ $N$ outcomes.
  • $\langle A\rangle=\bra\psi\hat A\ket\psi=\sum a_n|c_n|^2$ (ensemble average, need not be an eigenvalue). $\sigma_A^2=\langle A^2\rangle-\langle A\rangle^2$; $\sigma_A=0$ ⇔ eigenstate.
  • $\braket{\phi|\psi}$ (bar) = inner product; $\langle A\rangle$ (no bar) = average.
  • Superposition: add amplitudes, then square (cross term $2\,\mathrm{Re}(c_1^*c_2\ldots)$). Mixture: add probabilities. Only observables not commuting with the prep basis tell them apart.

2 · Dirac notation and linear algebra

  • Ket = column, bra = conjugate-transposed row. $\braket{\phi|\psi}=\sum\phi_n^*\psi_n=\int\phi^*\psi\,dx$; $\braket{\psi|\phi}=\braket{\phi|\psi}^*$.
  • Orthonormal $\braket{e_m|e_n}=\delta_{mn}$; completeness $\sum\ket{e_n}\bra{e_n}=\hat 1$; $A_{mn}=\bra{e_m}\hat A\ket{e_n}$ (row $m$, col $n$).
  • $A^\dagger=(A^T)^*$; $(AB)^\dagger=B^\dagger A^\dagger$, $(AB)^{-1}=B^{-1}A^{-1}$, $(\ket a\bra b)^\dagger=\ket b\bra a$.
  • Hermitian: real eigenvalues, orthogonal eigenvectors, complete basis, real averages. $AB$ Hermitian ⇔ $[A,B]=0$; $i[A,B]$ always Hermitian.
  • Unitary $U^\dagger U=1$: keeps lengths; $|\lambda|=1$; products unitary, sums not. $e^{i\theta\hat A}$ unitary for Hermitian $\hat A$.
  • Spectral: $\hat A=\sum a_n\ket{a_n}\bra{a_n}$, $f(\hat A)=\sum f(a_n)\ket{a_n}\bra{a_n}$. Projector $P=\ket e\bra e$: $P^2=P$, eigenvalues 0, 1.
  • 2×2: $\lambda=\tfrac12\left(\mathrm{tr}\pm\sqrt{\mathrm{tr}^2-4\det}\right)$; tr = Σλ, det = Πλ. Rows dependent ⇒ det 0 ⇒ no inverse.
  • Gram–Schmidt: $\ket{e_k'}\propto\ket{e_k}-\sum_{j\lt k}\braket{e_j'|e_k}\ket{e_j'}$ (conjugate on the bra). Cauchy–Schwarz $|\braket{v|w}|^2\le\braket{v|v}\braket{w|w}$.
  • $[A,BC]=[A,B]C+B[A,C]$. $[\hat x,\hat p]=i\hbar$, $[\hat x,\hat p^2]=2i\hbar\hat p$, $[f(\hat x),\hat p]=i\hbar f'$, $[\hat H,\hat x]=-\tfrac{i\hbar}{m}\hat p$.
  • Continuous: $\braket{x|x'}=\delta(x-x')$, $\Psi(x)=\braket{x|\psi}$, $\braket{x|p}=\tfrac{e^{ipx/\hbar}}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}$, $\Phi(p)=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt{2\pi\hbar}}\int e^{-ipx/\hbar}\Psi\,dx$. $\hat p\to-i\hbar\partial_x$ ($x$-space), $\hat x\to i\hbar\partial_p$ ($p$-space).

3 · Wavefunctions

  • $\int|\Psi|^2dx=1$ ($[\Psi]=L^{-1/2}$). $\langle f(x)\rangle=\int f|\Psi|^2$; $\langle p\rangle=\int\Psi^*(-i\hbar\Psi')$; $\langle p^2\rangle=\hbar^2\int|\Psi'|^2$ (safe at kinks).
  • Shortcuts: $|\Psi|^2$ symmetric about $x_0$ ⇒ $\langle x\rangle=x_0$; real Ψ (× const phase) ⇒ $\langle p\rangle=0$; factor $e^{ik_0x}$ adds $\hbar k_0$ to $\langle p\rangle$; time phases drop out of $|\Psi|^2$.
  • Current $J=\tfrac{\hbar}{m}\mathrm{Im}(\Psi^*\Psi')$; $\partial_t|\Psi|^2+\partial_xJ=0$; $Ae^{ikx}$: $J=|A|^2\hbar k/m$; real Ψ: $J=0$.
  • $\tfrac{d}{dt}\langle Q\rangle=\tfrac i\hbar\langle[\hat H,\hat Q]\rangle+\langle\partial_t\hat Q\rangle$. Ehrenfest: $\tfrac{d\langle x\rangle}{dt}=\tfrac{\langle p\rangle}{m}$, $\tfrac{d\langle p\rangle}{dt}=-\langle V'\rangle$ (exact Newton only if $V$ ≤ quadratic).

Integrals and identities

  • $\int_0^\infty x^ne^{-\alpha x}dx=\tfrac{n!}{\alpha^{n+1}}$ · $\int e^{-\alpha x^2-\beta x}dx=\sqrt{\tfrac\pi\alpha}e^{\beta^2/4\alpha}$ · $\int x^2e^{-\alpha x^2}=\tfrac{1}{2\alpha}\sqrt{\tfrac\pi\alpha}$
  • $\int_{-\infty}^\infty\tfrac{du}{(1+u^2)^2}=\int\tfrac{u^2du}{(1+u^2)^2}=\tfrac\pi2$; antiderivative $\tfrac12\left[\tfrac{u}{1+u^2}+\arctan u\right]$
  • $\int_0^a\sin^2\tfrac{n\pi x}a=\tfrac a2$ · $\int_0^ax\sin^2=\tfrac{a^2}4$ · $\int_0^ax^2\sin^2=a^3\left(\tfrac16-\tfrac{1}{4n^2\pi^2}\right)$
  • $\int_0^ax(a-x)\sin\tfrac{n\pi x}{a}dx=\tfrac{2a^3}{(n\pi)^3}[1-(-1)^n]$ · $\int_0^ax\,\psi_1\psi_2\,dx=-\tfrac{16a}{9\pi^2}$
  • $\sin A\sin B=\tfrac12[\cos(A-B)-\cos(A+B)]$ · $\sin^3u=\tfrac34\sin u-\tfrac14\sin3u$ · $\sin u\cos^2u=\tfrac14(\sin u+\sin3u)$
  • $|z_1+z_2|^2=|z_1|^2+|z_2|^2+2\mathrm{Re}(z_1^*z_2)$ · $e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$ · $Ae^{ikx}+Be^{-ikx}=(A+B)\cos kx+i(A-B)\sin kx$

4 · Stationary states and the recipe

  • $\Psi=\psi(x)e^{-iEt/\hbar}$, $\hat H\psi=E\psi$, $\hat H=-\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\partial_x^2+V$. Stationary: $|\Psi|^2$ and all $\langle Q\rangle$ frozen, $\sigma_H=0$.
  • Recipe: (1) solve $\hat H\psi_n=E_n\psi_n$; (2) $c_n=\int\psi_n^*\Psi(x,0)dx$; (3) $\Psi(x,t)=\sum c_n\psi_ne^{-iE_nt/\hbar}$. $P(E_n)=|c_n|^2$, $\langle H\rangle=\sum|c_n|^2E_n$: constant for any state.
  • $\langle H\rangle$ shortcut: $\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\int|\Psi'|^2+\int V|\Psi|^2$. Jumps in Ψ ⇒ $\langle H\rangle=\infty$; kinks ⇒ finite $\langle H\rangle$ but maybe $\sigma_H=\infty$.
  • Two energies ⇒ density beats at $\omega=(E_m-E_n)/\hbar$. Phase $e^{i\varphi}$ on $c_2$ shifts $\cos(\omega t)\to\cos(\omega t-\varphi)$.
  • $\psi$ continuous; $\psi'$ continuous unless $V=\infty$ (or δ). $E>V_{\min}$. Bound states: real, non-degenerate (1-D), $n$-th has $n-1$ nodes; even $V$ ⇒ even/odd, ground state even.
  • $\psi''=\tfrac{2m}{\hbar^2}(V-E)\psi$: $E>V$ oscillates (bigger $E-V$ ⇒ shorter λ, smaller amplitude); $E<V$ decays.
  • Inverse: $V(x)=E+\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}\tfrac{\psi''}{\psi}$; use the node-free ground state. $V(x_0)=E$ where $\psi''=0$.

5 · Infinite square well $[0,a]$

  • $\psi_n=\sqrt{\tfrac2a}\sin\tfrac{n\pi x}a$, $E_n=\tfrac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2ma^2}=n^2E_1$, $E_{n+1}-E_n=(2n+1)E_1$.
  • $\langle x\rangle=\tfrac a2$, $\langle x^2\rangle=a^2\left(\tfrac13-\tfrac1{2n^2\pi^2}\right)$, $\langle p^2\rangle=\left(\tfrac{n\pi\hbar}a\right)^2$, $\sigma_x\sigma_p=\tfrac\hbar2\sqrt{\tfrac{n^2\pi^2}3-2}$ (0.568ħ at $n=1$).
  • Centered $[-\tfrac a2,\tfrac a2]$: $\cos$ for odd $n$, $\sin$ for even; same $E_n$.
  • Symmetry: Ψ symmetric about $a/2$ ⇒ only odd $n$; antisymmetric ⇒ only even $n$.
  • $\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\psi_1+\psi_2)$: $\langle x\rangle=\tfrac a2-\tfrac{16a}{9\pi^2}\cos\omega t$, $\omega=\tfrac{3\pi^2\hbar}{2ma^2}$. Revival $T=\tfrac{4ma^2}{\pi\hbar}$.
  • Sudden change: Ψ unchanged, expand in new states; $\langle H\rangle$ unchanged. Ground state, wall $a\to2a$: $P(E_1')=\tfrac{32}{9\pi^2}$, $P(E_2')=\tfrac12$; both walls out symmetrically: $\tfrac{64}{9\pi^2}$.
  • Known $c_n$: $x(a-x)$: $\tfrac{8\sqrt{15}}{(n\pi)^3}$ (odd); triangle: $\tfrac{4\sqrt6}{(n\pi)^2}\sin\tfrac{n\pi}2$; left half flat: $\tfrac{2}{n\pi}(1-\cos\tfrac{n\pi}2)$.

6 · Free particle, Fourier, δ

  • $e^{ikx}$, $E=\tfrac{\hbar^2k^2}{2m}$: not normalizable. $\Psi(x,t)=\tfrac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\phi(k)e^{i(kx-\hbar k^2t/2m)}dk$, $\phi(k)=\tfrac1{\sqrt{2\pi}}\int\Psi(x,0)e^{-ikx}dx$; $\int|\phi|^2=\int|\Psi|^2$.
  • $v_g=\tfrac{d\omega}{dk}=\tfrac{\hbar k}{m}$ (particle), $v_{ph}=\tfrac{v_g}2$. $\sigma_p$ constant; packet spreads at $\sigma_p/m$.
  • Gaussian $(\tfrac{2a}\pi)^{1/4}e^{-ax^2}$: $\sigma_x=\tfrac1{2\sqrt a}$, $\sigma_p=\hbar\sqrt a$ (minimum), $\langle H\rangle=\tfrac{a\hbar^2}{2m}$ (+$\tfrac{\hbar^2k_0^2}{2m}$ if $e^{ik_0x}$); $\sigma(t)=\sigma_0\sqrt{1+(\tfrac{\hbar t}{2m\sigma_0^2})^2}$; $w=\sqrt a/\sqrt{1+(2\hbar at/m)^2}$, $|\Psi|^2=\sqrt{\tfrac2\pi}we^{-2w^2x^2}$.
  • Pairs: Gaussian↔Gaussian; box half-width $L$ ↔ $\tfrac{\sin kL}k$ (zeros $k=n\pi/L$); $\sqrt\kappa e^{-\kappa|x|}$ ↔ $\sqrt{\tfrac\kappa{2\pi}}\tfrac{2\kappa}{\kappa^2+k^2}$. $f e^{ik_0x}\leftrightarrow F(k-k_0)$; $f(x-x_0)\leftrightarrow e^{-ikx_0}F$. Narrow ⇔ wide.
  • $\int f\delta(x-a)=f(a)$; $\delta(cx)=\tfrac{\delta(x)}{|c|}$; $\tfrac1{2\pi}\int e^{ik(x-x')}dk=\delta(x-x')$; $(|x|)''=2\delta(x)$.

7 · Finite well ($-V_0$, $|x|<a$) and scattering

  • $\ell=\tfrac{\sqrt{2m(E+V_0)}}\hbar$, $\kappa=\tfrac{\sqrt{-2mE}}\hbar$, $z=\ell a$, $z_0=\tfrac a\hbar\sqrt{2mV_0}$, $E=-V_0+\tfrac{\hbar^2z^2}{2ma^2}$.
  • Even: $\kappa=\ell\tan\ell a$ ⇒ $\tan z=\sqrt{z_0^2/z^2-1}$. Odd: $\kappa=-\ell\cot\ell a$ ⇒ $-\cot z=\sqrt{\cdot}$ (needs $z_0>\tfrac\pi2$).
  • $N=\lceil2z_0/\pi\rceil$; always ≥1 even state. Deep: $E_n+V_0\approx\tfrac{n^2\pi^2\hbar^2}{2m(2a)^2}$ (slightly lower: leakage). $P_{\text{out}}=\tfrac{\cos^2z/\kappa a}{1+\frac{\sin2z}{2z}+\cos^2z/\kappa a}$.
  • Penetration $1/\kappa$; electron, 1 eV below rim: 0.195 nm.
  • Well, $E>0$: $T^{-1}=1+\tfrac{V_0^2}{4E(E+V_0)}\sin^2(2a\ell)$; $T=1$ when $2a\ell=n\pi$.
  • Step ($E>V_0$) or drop: $R=\left(\tfrac{k-k'}{k+k'}\right)^2$, $T=\tfrac{4kk'}{(k+k')^2}=\tfrac{k'}k|t|^2$ (flux). $E<V_0$ step: $R=1$, evanescent tail, $J=0$.
  • Barrier $E<V_0$: $T^{-1}=1+\tfrac{V_0^2\sinh^2\kappa L}{4E(V_0-E)}$; $\kappa L\gg1$: $T\approx\tfrac{16E(V_0-E)}{V_0^2}e^{-2\kappa L}$. $E>V_0$: $\sinh\to\sin$, $\kappa\to k'$; $T=1$ at $k'L=n\pi$. Compare $L$ with $1/\kappa$ (or $1/k'$).

8 · Uncertainty

  • $\sigma_A^2\sigma_B^2\ge\left(\tfrac1{2i}\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right)^2$ (uses the average commutator: can vanish). $\sigma_x\sigma_p\ge\tfrac\hbar2$ always. Commuting ⇒ common eigenbasis.
  • Equality: Gaussians, at minimum width. Spreads of the state, not measurement kicks.
  • $\sigma_H\Delta t\ge\tfrac\hbar2$, $\Delta t=\sigma_Q/|d\langle Q\rangle/dt|$; time is not an operator. $\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(\psi_1+\psi_2)$: $\sigma_H=\tfrac{E_2-E_1}2$.
  • Estimate: $\langle H\rangle\gtrsim\tfrac{\hbar^2}{8m\sigma^2}+\langle V\rangle$, minimize. HO → $\tfrac12\hbar\omega$ (exact); H atom → $a_0$, −13.6 eV.
  • $\sigma_x\sigma_H\ge\tfrac\hbar{2m}|\langle p\rangle|$. Free: $\sigma_x^2(t)=\sigma_x^2(0)+\tfrac{\sigma_p^2t^2}{m^2}$ (no initial $x$–$p$ correlation).

9 · Qubit / spin-½

  • $\sigma_x=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $\sigma_y=\begin{pmatrix}0&-i\\i&0\end{pmatrix}$, $\sigma_z=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}$: Hermitian, unitary, traceless, $\sigma^2=1$, eigenvalues ±1. $\vec S=\tfrac\hbar2\vec\sigma$.
  • $\sigma_j\sigma_k=\delta_{jk}+i\epsilon_{jkl}\sigma_l$; $[\sigma_j,\sigma_k]=2i\epsilon_{jkl}\sigma_l$; $(\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma)(\vec b\cdot\vec\sigma)=\vec a\cdot\vec b+i(\vec a\times\vec b)\cdot\vec\sigma$.
  • $\ket\psi=\cos\tfrac\theta2\ket0+e^{i\varphi}\sin\tfrac\theta2\ket1$ ↔ $\hat n$; $\langle\vec\sigma\rangle=\hat n$; orthogonal = antipodal. $\ket\pm=\tfrac{\ket0\pm\ket1}{\sqrt2}$, $\ket{\pm y}=\tfrac{\ket0\pm i\ket1}{\sqrt2}$.
  • $P(+\hat m)=\tfrac12(1+\hat n\cdot\hat m)=\cos^2\tfrac\gamma2$. $\Delta\sigma_j=\sqrt{1-\langle\sigma_j\rangle^2}$.
  • $H=a_0+\vec a\cdot\vec\sigma$: $E=a_0\pm|\vec a|$, eigenstates along $\pm\hat a$. $e^{i\alpha\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma}=\cos\alpha+i\sin\alpha\,\hat n\cdot\vec\sigma$; $2\pi$ rotation = −1.
  • $\hat U=e^{-i\hat Ht/\hbar}$. $\hat H=\tfrac\hbar2\vec\Omega\cdot\vec\sigma$ precesses $\hat n$ about $\vec\Omega$ at rate $|\vec\Omega|$. From $\ket0$ with $\hbar\omega(\alpha\sigma_z+\beta\sigma_x)$: $P_1=\tfrac{\beta^2}{R^2}\sin^2(\omega Rt)$, $R=\sqrt{\alpha^2+\beta^2}$. $\tfrac{\hbar\Omega}2\sigma_x$: $P_1=\sin^2\tfrac{\Omega t}2$. Ramsey: $P_1=\cos^2\tfrac{\omega T}2$.

Traps

  • $|z|^2=z^*z$, not $z^2$. Conjugate when forming a bra.
  • Normalize first; keep $\sqrt{2/a}$ in $c_n$; each $E_n$ its own phase.
  • Cusp: $\langle p^2\rangle$ from $\int|\Psi'|^2$, not $\Psi''$ (δ at the kink).
  • Bloch angles are doubled: $\cos^2(\gamma/2)$, antipodal = orthogonal.
  • $T$ is a flux ratio ($k'/k$); thick-barrier formula needs $\kappa L\gg1$. A "probability" with units ⇒ non-normalizable state.
