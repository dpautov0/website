1 · Dynamics of expectation values

  • Generalized Ehrenfest: $\tfrac{d}{dt}\langle Q\rangle=\tfrac i\hbar\langle[\hat H,\hat Q]\rangle+\langle\partial_t\hat Q\rangle$. Commutes with $\hat H$ (no explicit $t$) ⇒ conserved.
  • $[\hat A\hat B,\hat C]=\hat A[\hat B,\hat C]+[\hat A,\hat C]\hat B$ · $[f(\hat x),\hat p]=i\hbar f'$ · $[\hat x,\hat p^2]=2i\hbar\hat p$ · $[\hat H,\hat x]=-\tfrac{i\hbar}m\hat p$ · $[\hat H,\hat p]=i\hbar V'$.
  • $Q=1$: norm conserved; $\hat H$: energy; $\hat x$: $\tfrac{d\langle x\rangle}{dt}=\tfrac{\langle p\rangle}m$; $\hat p$: $\tfrac{d\langle p\rangle}{dt}=-\langle V'\rangle$ (Newton only if $V$ ≤ quadratic).
  • Virial: $\tfrac{d}{dt}\langle xp\rangle=2\langle T\rangle-\langle xV'\rangle=0$ in stationary states. $V\propto x^2$: $\langle T\rangle=\langle V\rangle$; $V\propto-1/r$: $\langle T\rangle=-E$, $\langle V\rangle=2E$.
  • Stationary state: every $\langle Q\rangle$ constant (no explicit $t$). Parity: even $V$ ⇒ $\hat\Pi$ conserved.

2 · Uncertainty, energy and time

  • $\sigma_A\sigma_B\ge\left|\tfrac1{2i}\langle[\hat A,\hat B]\rangle\right|$ · $\sigma_H\sigma_Q\ge\tfrac\hbar2\left|\tfrac{d\langle Q\rangle}{dt}\right|$ ⇒ $\Delta E\Delta t\ge\tfrac\hbar2$, $\Delta t=\tfrac{\sigma_Q}{|d\langle Q\rangle/dt|}$.
  • $\sigma_x\sigma_H\ge\tfrac\hbar{2m}|\langle p\rangle|$: empty for stationary states (both sides 0). Lifetime $\tau$ ⇒ linewidth $\sim\hbar/\tau$. Fast change needs big $\sigma_H$.
  • Equality iff $(\hat B-\langle B\rangle)\psi=i\lambda(\hat A-\langle A\rangle)\psi$, $\lambda$ real (Schwarz equality + $\mathrm{Re}\,z=0$). $x,p$: Gaussian, $\sigma_x^2=\hbar/2\lambda$. Free Gaussian leaves the bound as $\mathrm{Re}\,z=\tfrac12\langle xp+px\rangle-\langle x\rangle\langle p\rangle$ grows. ISW can't reach $\hbar/2$ (no Gaussian vanishes at walls).

3 · Oscillator: algebra

  • $\hat a_\pm=\tfrac1{\sqrt{2\hbar m\omega}}(\mp i\hat p+m\omega\hat x)$, $[\hat a_-,\hat a_+]=1$, $\hat H=\hbar\omega(\hat a_+\hat a_-+\tfrac12)$, $\hat a_\pm^\dagger=\hat a_\mp$.
  • $\hat H\hat a_\pm\psi=(E\pm\hbar\omega)\hat a_\pm\psi$; ladder ends: $\hat a_-\psi_0=0$ ⇒ $E_n=\hbar\omega(n+\tfrac12)$, $n=0,1,\dots$; evenly spaced.
  • $\hat a_+\ket n=\sqrt{n+1}\ket{n+1}$, $\hat a_-\ket n=\sqrt n\ket{n-1}$, $\ket n=\tfrac{(\hat a_+)^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\ket0$, $\hat N=\hat a_+\hat a_-$.
  • $\hat x=\sqrt{\tfrac\hbar{2m\omega}}(\hat a_++\hat a_-)$, $\hat p=i\sqrt{\tfrac{\hbar m\omega}2}(\hat a_+-\hat a_-)$. x, p: n→n±1; x², p²: n→n, n±2.
  • $\ket n$: $\langle x^2\rangle=(n+\tfrac12)\tfrac\hbar{m\omega}$, $\langle p^2\rangle=(n+\tfrac12)\hbar m\omega$, $\sigma_x\sigma_p=(n+\tfrac12)\hbar$, $\langle T\rangle=\langle V\rangle=\tfrac{E_n}2$.
  • $\bra{n+1}\hat x\ket n=\sqrt{\tfrac{(n+1)\hbar}{2m\omega}}$; $\bra{n+2}\hat x^2\ket n=\tfrac{\hbar}{2m\omega}\sqrt{(n+1)(n+2)}$.

4 · Oscillator: wavefunctions and motion

  • $\psi_0=(\tfrac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar})^{1/4}e^{-m\omega x^2/2\hbar}$; $\psi_n\propto H_n(\xi)e^{-\xi^2/2}$, $\xi=\sqrt{\tfrac{m\omega}\hbar}x$, norm $(\tfrac{m\omega}{\pi\hbar})^{1/4}(2^nn!)^{-1/2}$. $H_1=2\xi$, $H_2=4\xi^2-2$, $H_3=8\xi^3-12\xi$.
  • $n$ nodes, parity $(-1)^n$, tails beyond turning point $x_n=\sqrt{2n+1}\,w_x$, $w_x=\sqrt{\hbar/m\omega}$. Large $n$ → $P_{cl}=\tfrac1{\pi\sqrt{x_0^2-x^2}}$ (peaks at the turning points).
  • Half oscillator (wall at 0): odd $n$ only, $E=\tfrac32,\tfrac72,\dots\hbar\omega$, $\psi=\sqrt2\psi_{odd}$. $V+qEx$: complete the square, shift by $-qE/m\omega^2$, $E_n$ lowered by $\tfrac{q^2E^2}{2m\omega^2}$.
  • $c_0\ket0+c_1\ket1$: $\langle x\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{2\hbar}{m\omega}}|c_0c_1|\cos(\omega t-\theta)$; phases: $\langle H\rangle$ gives $|c_n|$, $\langle x\rangle$ the relative phase, $\mathrm{sign}\,\langle p\rangle$ its sign.
  • Phase space $(\langle x\rangle,\langle p\rangle/m\omega)$: clockwise circle, period $2\pi/\omega$. $\ket n$: a point at 0. $\ket0\pm\ket2$: $\langle x\rangle=0$, width breathes at $2\omega$.
  • Classical iff $n\gg1$ and amplitude $x_0\gg w_x$ ($x_0/w_x=\sqrt{2n+1}$).

5 · Coherent states

  • $\hat a_-\ket\alpha=\alpha\ket\alpha$, $\ket\alpha=e^{-|\alpha|^2/2}\sum\tfrac{\alpha^n}{\sqrt{n!}}\ket n$, $P(n)=e^{-|\alpha|^2}\tfrac{|\alpha|^{2n}}{n!}$ (Poisson).
  • $\langle n\rangle=|\alpha|^2$, $\sigma_n=|\alpha|$, $\sigma_H/\langle H\rangle\to1/|\alpha|$. $\alpha(t)=\alpha e^{-i\omega t}$: stays coherent.
  • $\langle x\rangle=\sqrt{\tfrac{2\hbar}{m\omega}}\mathrm{Re}\,\alpha$, $\langle p\rangle=\sqrt{2\hbar m\omega}\,\mathrm{Im}\,\alpha$; $\sigma_x=\sqrt{\tfrac\hbar{2m\omega}}$, $\sigma_p=\sqrt{\tfrac{\hbar m\omega}2}$ always: minimum uncertainty, ground-state blob riding the classical orbit. $\ket0=\ket{\alpha=0}$.
  • vs $\ket N$ ($|\alpha|^2=N$): same $\langle H\rangle$; $\ket N$ has $\sigma_H=0$ and doesn't move. Motion needs adjacent $n$ with fixed relative phases; price: $\sigma_H=\hbar\omega|\alpha|$.

Exam moves: see this → do this

  • Superposition of $\ket n$ → write $\hat x\ket\psi$ with ladders; only $n,n\pm1$ survive; frequencies = energy differences.
  • Known system, two states → it's a qubit: $\sigma_z$ = which level, $\sigma_x$ = which side.
  • Function of θ, φ → Cartesian shapes: $x\pm iy$, $z$, $(x\pm iy)^2$ → read off $\ell,m$; $e^{im\phi}$ alone gives $L_z$.
  • Measure $L_x$ in ℓ=1 → $3\times3$ matrix, eigenvectors $\tfrac12(1,\pm\sqrt2,1)$, $\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,-1)$.
  • Hermitian? → by parts; say which boundary condition kills the boundary term.
  • Central $V$ → $u=rR$, sketch $V_{\rm eff}$, mark allowed regions, count nodes.
  • T/F → verdict + the principle (conservation, $u(0)=0$, bound ⇔ $E\lt V(\infty)$, collapse, compatibility). Verdict alone = 1/5.

6 · Three dimensions

  • $\hat{\vec p}=-i\hbar\nabla$; $\nabla^2=\tfrac1{r^2}\partial_r(r^2\partial_r)-\tfrac{\hat L^2}{\hbar^2r^2}$; $d^3r=r^2\sin\theta\,dr\,d\theta\,d\phi$, $d\Omega=\sin\theta\,d\theta\,d\phi$.
  • Central $V(r)$: $\psi=R(r)Y_\ell^m$, separation constant $\ell(\ell+1)$; $\int|R|^2r^2dr=1$, $\int|Y|^2d\Omega=1$.
  • Cube: $E\propto n_x^2+n_y^2+n_z^2$. Isotropic SHO: $E=\hbar\omega(N+\tfrac32)$, deg. $\tfrac{(N+1)(N+2)}2$, $\ell=N,N-2,\dots$ (N=2: $\ell=0$ + $\ell=2$: accidental).
  • Classically $\vec L$ fixed ⇒ plane (3D→2D), $|L|$ fixed ⇒ radial 1D. QM keeps only the second ($\hat L^2$ sharp, direction not).

7 · Angular momentum algebra

  • $\hat L_z=\hat x\hat p_y-\hat y\hat p_x$ (cyclic). $[\hat L_x,\hat L_y]=i\hbar\hat L_z$, $[\hat L^2,\hat L_i]=0$, $[\hat L_z,\hat x]=i\hbar\hat y$, $[\hat L_z,\hat y]=-i\hbar\hat x$, $[\hat L_z,\hat z]=0$ (same with $\hat p$). $[\hat L_z,r^2]=[\hat L_z,p^2]=0$.
  • Only one component sharp (unless $\ell=0$). Compatible set $\{\hat H,\hat L^2,\hat L_z\}$ for central $V$; $\tfrac{d}{dt}\langle\vec L\rangle=\langle\vec r\times(-\nabla V)\rangle$.
  • $\hat L_\pm=\hat L_x\pm i\hat L_y$, $\hat L_\pm^\dagger=\hat L_\mp$, $[\hat L_z,\hat L_\pm]=\pm\hbar\hat L_\pm$, $\hat L_\mp\hat L_\pm=\hat L^2-\hat L_z^2\mp\hbar\hat L_z$.
  • $\hat L^2=\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)$, $L_z=\hbar m$, $m=-\ell..\ell$; $\hat L_\pm Y_\ell^m=\hbar\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)-m(m\pm1)}\,Y_\ell^{m\pm1}$; $\hat L_+Y_\ell^\ell=0$.
  • Vector model: $|L|=\hbar\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)}>\hbar\ell$; cone angle $\cos\theta=m/\sqrt{\ell(\ell+1)}$; $\langle L_x^2\rangle=\langle L_y^2\rangle=\tfrac{\hbar^2}2[\ell(\ell+1)-m^2]$ in $Y_\ell^m$.
  • $\ell$ bounds $m$; $m$ only gives $\ell\ge|m|$.

8 · Spherical harmonics, measuring L

  • $\hat L_z=-i\hbar\partial_\phi$ ⇒ $e^{im\phi}$, $m\in\mathbb Z$ (single-valued). $\hat L_\pm=\pm\hbar e^{\pm i\phi}(\partial_\theta\pm i\cot\theta\,\partial_\phi)$ ⇒ $Y_\ell^\ell\propto\sin^\ell\theta\,e^{i\ell\phi}$.
  • $Y_0^0=\tfrac1{\sqrt{4\pi}}$, $Y_1^0=\sqrt{\tfrac3{4\pi}}\cos\theta$, $Y_1^{\pm1}=\mp\sqrt{\tfrac3{8\pi}}\sin\theta e^{\pm i\phi}$, $Y_2^0=\sqrt{\tfrac5{16\pi}}(3\cos^2\theta-1)$, $Y_2^{\pm1}=\mp\sqrt{\tfrac{15}{8\pi}}\sin\theta\cos\theta e^{\pm i\phi}$, $Y_2^{\pm2}=\sqrt{\tfrac{15}{32\pi}}\sin^2\theta e^{\pm2i\phi}$. Parity $(-1)^\ell$.
  • $\ell=1$ basis $(1,0,-1)$: $L_x=\tfrac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&1&0\\1&0&1\\0&1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $L_y=\tfrac\hbar{\sqrt2}\begin{pmatrix}0&-i&0\\i&0&-i\\0&i&0\end{pmatrix}$. $L_x$: $\pm\hbar$: $\tfrac12(1,\pm\sqrt2,1)$; $0$: $\tfrac1{\sqrt2}(1,0,-1)$. From $Y_1^{\pm1}$: $P(L_x=\hbar,0,-\hbar)=\tfrac14,\tfrac12,\tfrac14$; from $Y_1^0$: $\tfrac12,0,\tfrac12$.
  • Successive: measuring $L_x$ erases $L_z$; $L^2$ survives. Probabilities need the full state, not just $\ell$.
  • Rotor $\hat H=\tfrac{\hat L^2}{2I}$: $E_\ell=\tfrac{\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)}{2I}$, gaps $\tfrac{\hbar^2}{I}(\ell+1)$, deg. $2\ell+1$. $I_z\ne I$: $E=\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2I}[\ell(\ell+1)-\tfrac{\epsilon}{1+\epsilon}m^2]$, $\pm m$ stay degenerate; $-\gamma B\hat L_z$ splits them.

9 · Radial equation, $V_{\rm eff}$, box

  • $-\tfrac{\hbar^2}{2m}u''+V_{\rm eff}u=Eu$, $V_{\rm eff}=V+\tfrac{\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)}{2mr^2}$; $u(0)=0$ (ℓ=0: $1/r$ would give a $\delta^3$); $|u|^2dr$ = radial probability.
  • Near 0: $u\propto r^{\ell+1}$, $R\propto r^\ell$: only s-states have $\psi(0)\ne0$. Bound: $u\to0$, discrete; $E>V(\infty)$: oscillates, continuous. $+k/r$: no bound states.
  • Spherical box $b$: $E=\tfrac{\hbar^2\beta^2}{2mb^2}$; ℓ=0: $u=\sin kr$, $\beta=N\pi$. Lowest ℓ ground state (centrifugal term > 0). Order 1s 1p 1d 2s 1f; magic 2, 8, 20.

10 · Hydrogen

  • $V=-\tfrac kr$, $a=\tfrac{\hbar^2}{mk}=0.0529$ nm, $E_n=-\tfrac{k}{2an^2}=-\tfrac{13.6\text{ eV}}{n^2}$, $n=N+\ell$, $\ell\le n-1$; deg. $n^2$ (2ℓ+1 rotational, rest accidental: hidden $1/r$ symmetry).
  • $\psi_{100}=\tfrac{e^{-r/a}}{\sqrt{\pi a^3}}$; $P(r)=\tfrac{4r^2}{a^3}e^{-2r/a}$, peak $a$, $\langle r\rangle=\tfrac32a$, $\langle\tfrac1r\rangle=\tfrac1a$; $P(r\lt b)=1-e^{-2b/a}(1+\tfrac{2b}a+\tfrac{2b^2}{a^2})$, $P(r\lt a)=0.323$.
  • $R_{20}=\tfrac{1}{\sqrt2a^{3/2}}(1-\tfrac r{2a})e^{-r/2a}$, $R_{21}=\tfrac1{\sqrt{24}a^{3/2}}\tfrac rae^{-r/2a}$. $\langle r\rangle=\tfrac a2[3n^2-\ell(\ell+1)]$, $\langle\tfrac1r\rangle=\tfrac1{n^2a}$.
  • $V_{\rm eff}$ min at $r=\ell(\ell+1)a$, depth $E_1/\ell(\ell+1)$. $\lambda=\tfrac{1240\text{ eV nm}}{\Delta E}$: Lyman α 122 nm; Balmer 656, 486, 434 nm.
  • Extra $-\tfrac{\hbar^2\alpha^2}{2mr^2}$ (alkali core): $\ell\to\lambda_\ell$, $E=\tfrac{E_1}{(n-\delta_\ell)^2}$, $\delta_\ell\approx\tfrac{\alpha^2}{2\ell+1}$: s lowest (penetrates).

Traps

  • $\hat a_-\ket0=0$, not $\ket{-1}$; $n$ starts at 0. $\hat a_\pm$ not Hermitian.
  • $\hat x\ket n$ has two terms; $\langle x^2\rangle$ needs $\hat a_-\hat a_+$ and $\hat a_+\hat a_-$ (they differ by 1).
  • $|\vec L|^2=\hbar^2\ell(\ell+1)$, not $\hbar^2\ell^2$. $Y_1^{\pm1}$ carries $\mp$.
  • $d\Omega$ has $\sin\theta$; $r^2|R|^2$ (not $|R|^2$) is the radial density.
  • Energy can't leak or be "lost" with time-independent $\hat H$; bound needs $E\lt V(\infty)$.
