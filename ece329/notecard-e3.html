Phasors, no calculator

$X(t) = \Re\{\tilde Xe^{j\omega t}\}$ | $\partial_t \to j\omega$ | $\cos \to 1$, $\sin \to -j$, $-\cos \to -1$

$\frac1j = -j$ | $\sqrt j = e^{j45°} = \frac{1+j}{\sqrt2}$ | $\sqrt{-j} = \frac{1-j}{\sqrt2}$ | $e^{j\pi} = -1$

$\cos x = \frac{e^{jx} + e^{-jx}}{2}$ | $\sin x = \frac{e^{jx} - e^{-jx}}{2j}$

divide: × conjugate | polar: sizes ÷, angles −

$\langle\Re\{Ae^{j\omega t}\}\,\Re\{Be^{j\omega t}\}\rangle = \frac12\Re\{AB^*\}$

triangles 1:1:$\sqrt2$ → 45° | 1:$\sqrt3$:2 → 30°, 60° | 3:4:5 → 36.9°, 53.1°

Lossy medium

$x = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon} = \frac{60\,\sigma\lambda_0}{\epsilon_r}$ (conduction ÷ displacement): $x < 0.1$ low loss, $x > 10$ conductor, else exact

method: $k = \omega\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$, $\eta_i = \sqrt{\mu/\epsilon}$; $1 - jx = \rho e^{-j\theta}$

$\gamma = jk\sqrt\rho\,e^{-j\theta/2}$ | $\eta = \frac{\eta_i}{\sqrt\rho}e^{j\theta/2}$ | $\tau = \frac\theta2 \le 45°$

check: $\angle\gamma + \angle\eta = 90°$, $|\gamma||\eta| = \omega\mu$

exact: $\beta^2 - \alpha^2 = k^2$ | $2\alpha\beta = \omega\mu\sigma$

conductor: $\delta = \frac1\alpha = \frac{1}{\sqrt{\pi f\mu\sigma}}$ | $\gamma = \frac{1+j}{\delta}$ | $\eta = \frac{1+j}{\sigma\delta}$

inside: $\lambda = 2\pi\delta$, $v_p = \omega\delta$; at $z = \delta$: field 37%, power 13.5%

$H$ lags $E$ by $\tau$; both decay as $e^{-\alpha z}$

Reading a wave

$E_0e^{-\alpha z}\cos(\omega t - \beta z + \phi)$: $\lambda = \frac{2\pi}\beta$, $v_p = \frac\omega\beta$, $\frac1\alpha$ = the $\frac1e$ distance

H from E: size ÷ $|\eta|$, phase $-\tau$, direction $\hat k\times\hat e$

$\tan\tau = \frac\alpha\beta$ (because $\angle\gamma = 90° - \tau$)

Cu $\sigma = 5.8\times10^7$: $\delta \approx 66\ \mu$m at 1 MHz, $\delta \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt f}$ | sea: $\sigma \approx 4$, $\epsilon_r \approx 81$

Power and loss

$\langle S\rangle = \frac{E_0^2}{2|\eta|}\cos\tau\,e^{-2\alpha z} = \frac12|H_0|^2\Re\{\eta\}\,e^{-2\alpha z}$

lossless $\frac{E_0^2}{2\eta}$ | field $e^{-\alpha z}$, power $e^{-2\alpha z}$

dB $= 10\log\frac{P_1}{P_2} = 8.69\,\alpha z$ | 3 dB = ½, 10 dB = $\frac1{10}$, 20 dB = $\frac1{100}$

heat per volume $\frac12\sigma|\tilde E|^2 = -\nabla\cdot\langle\mathbf S\rangle$

$R_s = \frac{1}{\sigma\delta} = \sqrt{\frac{\pi f\mu}{\sigma}}$ | conductor $\eta = (1+j)R_s$

loss/area $\frac12R_s|\tilde J_s|^2$, $\tilde{\mathbf J}_s = \hat n\times\tilde{\mathbf H}$ (mirror: $|\tilde J_s| = 2|\tilde H_i|$); mirror absorbs $\frac{4R_s}{\eta_0}$

pressure: mirror $\frac{2\langle S_i\rangle}{c}$, absorber $\frac{\langle S_i\rangle}{c}$ (N/m²)

$\langle w_e\rangle = \frac14\epsilon|\tilde E|^2$, $\langle w_m\rangle = \frac14\mu|\tilde H|^2$; lossless: equal, $\langle S\rangle = v_p(\langle w_e\rangle + \langle w_m\rangle)$

$-\nabla\cdot\mathbf S = \partial_t(w_e + w_m) + \mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E$: both terms; on average only $\mathbf J\cdot\mathbf E$ is left

Polarization

linear: in phase or 180° | circular: equal sizes, ±90° | else elliptical

hand: $\tilde{\mathbf E} = \mathbf a + j\mathbf b$ at one plane: $\mathbf E$ turns $\mathbf a \to -\mathbf b$; spin axis $\mathbf b\times\mathbf a$ along travel: RH, against: LH

right thumb along travel, fingers turn with $\mathbf E$ → RH

$+z$: $\hat x - j\hat y$ RH | $\hat x + j\hat y$ LH | $\cos\psi\,\hat x + \sin\psi\,\hat y$ RH ($\psi = \omega t - \beta z$)

reflection (real Γ) flips the hand

$\langle S\rangle = \frac{|\tilde E_x|^2 + |\tilde E_y|^2}{2\eta}$: the two parts add

Launch, reflect, steady state

$V^+ = \tau_gV_g$ (line looks like $Z_0$) | $I^\pm = \pm\frac{V^\pm}{Z_0}$ | current reflects with $-\Gamma$

Γ: matched 0, short −1, open +1; $R > Z_0$ ⇒ $\Gamma > 0$

$V_L = (1 + \Gamma_L)V^+$ | $I_L = (1 - \Gamma_L)\frac{V^+}{Z_0}$

joint $Z_a \to Z_b$: $\Gamma = \frac{Z_b - Z_a}{Z_b + Z_a}$, $\tau = 1 + \Gamma$; from b's side: $-\Gamma$, $1 - \Gamma$

two lines after the joint: $Z_b\|Z_c$ | resistor at the joint: $R\|Z_b$

steady state (DC): the line vanishes: $V = V_g\frac{R_L}{R_g + R_L}$, $I = \frac{V_g}{R_g + R_L}$; $= \tau_gV_g\frac{1 + \Gamma_L}{1 - \Gamma_L\Gamma_g}$

Stubs and resonance

short: $Z = jZ_0\tan\beta l$: under $\frac\lambda4$ → L; 0 at $l = n\frac\lambda2$, ∞ at $l = (2n - 1)\frac\lambda4$

open: $Z = -jZ_0\cot\beta l$: under $\frac\lambda4$ → C; ∞ at $n\frac\lambda2$, 0 at $(2n - 1)\frac\lambda4$

in $f$ ($\beta l = \frac{2\pi fl}{v}$): short stub 0 at $f = \frac{nv}{2l}$, ∞ at $f = \frac{(2n - 1)v}{4l}$; open: swap

short, $\beta l \ll 1$: $L = \mathcal Ll$ | open: $C = \mathcal Cl$

shunt stub that is 0 at $f$: notch (kills $f$) | ∞ at $f$: invisible | series stub ∞: blocks

ringing line: same ends (S-S, O-O) $f = \frac{nv}{2l}$, period $\frac{2l}v$ | mixed (S-O) $f = \frac{(2n - 1)v}{4l}$, period $\frac{4l}v$

Speed rules and numbers

1. lossless numbers first, then $x = \frac{\sigma}{\omega\epsilon}$, then the matching sheet line

2. $\tilde{\mathbf H} = \frac1\eta\hat k\times\tilde{\mathbf E}$ | $\tilde{\mathbf E} = \eta\tilde{\mathbf H}\times\hat k$ ($\hat k$ = travel)

3. sign of Γ first: smaller η or R ahead ⇒ $\Gamma < 0$ ⇒ minimum at the boundary or load

4. $\frac\lambda4$ inverts, $\frac\lambda2$ repeats; short under $\frac\lambda4$ = L, open under $\frac\lambda4$ = C

5. last bounce level = the DC divider: check it

6. a joint: $V$ continuous and currents add (that is $\tau = 1 + \Gamma$)

7. three τ's: angle of η | $1 + \Gamma$ | $\tau_g = \frac{Z_0}{R_g + Z_0}$: name which one
