ECE 329 · Exam 2 · FRONT$\mu_0 = 4\pi\times10^{-7}$ · $\epsilon_0 = 8.854\times10^{-12}$ · $\eta_0 = 120\pi$ · not on the official sheet

1 · Signs and directions

  • Fingers along $C$, thumb $= d\mathbf S$. $\mathcal E$, $I > 0$: along $C$.
  • Lenz: $I$ fights the change of $\Psi$: a check, not a second minus.
  • Wire: thumb along $I$, fingers $=\mathbf B$. Loop: fingers along $I$, thumb $=\mathbf B$ inside.
  • Sheet: $\mathbf H = \tfrac12\mathbf J_s\times\hat n$, $\hat n$ from the sheet to the point; $\mathbf H^+ - \mathbf H^- = \mathbf J_s\times\hat n_{-\to+}$.
  • Parallel currents attract. Sheet force: $\mathbf J_s\times\mathbf B_{\text{others}}$ (average of both sides).
  • $\mathbf E\times\mathbf H$ along the travel; $f(t - z/v)$ and $\omega t - \beta z$ go to $+z$.

2 · Faraday in 5 steps, and voltmeters

  • (1) pick $C$; RHR: $d\mathbf S$ (2) $\Psi = \int\mathbf B\cdot d\mathbf S$ where $B \ne 0$ (3) $\mathcal E = -N\dot\Psi$ (4) $I = \mathcal E/R_{\text{tot}}$ (5) Lenz.
  • Voltmeter reads $\int_+^-\mathbf E\cdot d\mathbf l$ along its leads. Leads + part of the circuit make a loop: $V + \sum(\pm IR) = -\dot\Psi_{\text{encl}}$ ($d\mathbf S$ by RHR on that loop); $+IR$ walking with $I$.
  • Hugging a resistor: $\pm IR$. Square's diagonal: half the flux. Two routes around the flux differ by $\mathcal E$. Symmetric ring, diameter: 0.

3 · Motional emf

  • $\mathcal E = \oint(\mathbf v\times\mathbf B)\cdot d\mathbf l$ along $C$. Bar: $vB\ell$, $B$ at the bar; $\mathbf v\times\mathbf B$ pushes $+q$.
  • Rails: $I = vB\ell/R$, $F = I\ell B$ against $\mathbf v$, $P = Fv = I^2R$.
  • Square in $B_0(1 + x/L)\hat z$ at $v\hat x$: $\mathcal E = -s^2B_0v/L$, constant; moving along $y$: 0.
  • Rod $\tfrac12B\omega L^2$. Solenoid ($a$, $\dot B$): $E_\phi = -\tfrac r2\dot B$ in, $-\tfrac{a^2}{2r}\dot B$ out.

4 · Fields you must know

  • $H$: solenoid $nI$; toroid core $NI/2\pi r$; coax $I/2\pi r$; thick wire $Ir/2\pi a^2$; zero outside the first three.
  • $d\mathbf B = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{I\,d\mathbf l\times\hat R}{R^2}$, $d\mathbf F = I\,d\mathbf l\times\mathbf B$. Loop axis $\frac{\mu_0Ia^2}{2(a^2 + z^2)^{3/2}}$, center $\frac{\mu_0I}{2a}$. Wires $\frac{\mu_0I_1I_2}{2\pi d}$ N/m.
  • Sheets $\pm J_s$: $H = J_s$ between, 0 out. Slab: a sheet $JW$ outside, $dH_y/dz = -J_x$ inside. Net $K$: far field $\pm K/2$.
  • $\delta(z)$ is 1/m: $K\delta(z - a)$ = sheet, $I\delta(x)\delta(y)$ = wire, $u(z - a) - u(z - b)$ = slab. $\frac{d}{dz}|z| = \operatorname{sgn}z$.

5 · Inductance

  • $I$ → $\mathbf H$ (free $I$) → $\mathbf B = \mu\mathbf H$ → $\Psi$ of one turn → $L = N\Psi/I$.
  • Solenoid $\frac{\mu N^2A}{\ell}$; toroid $\frac{\mu N^2h}{2\pi}\ln\frac ba$; coax $L' = \frac{\mu}{2\pi}\ln\frac ba$; pp $L' = \frac{\mu d}{w}$; layers: $\sum\frac{\mu_i}{2\pi}\ln\frac{r_{i+1}}{r_i}$.
  • $L'C' = \mu\epsilon$. $M = \Psi_{21}/I_1 = M_{12}$; one turn on the core: $L/N$. $\tfrac12LI^2$.
  • $L$ never depends on $I$; $\propto N^2$; all lengths $\times k$: $L\times k$, $L'$ same.

6 · C, G, relaxation

  • pp $\frac{\epsilon A}{d}$; coax $C' = \frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln(b/a)}$; spheres $\frac{4\pi\epsilon ab}{b - a}$, alone $4\pi\epsilon a$; $R = \frac{\ell}{\sigma A}$.
  • $G/C = \sigma/\epsilon$, $\tau = RC = \epsilon/\sigma$. In DC a lossy C is just $1/G$.
  • Stacked: same $D$, $V = D\sum\frac{d_i}{\epsilon_i}$. Side by side: same $E$, C's add.
  • Leaky, steady: $\sigma_1E_1 = \sigma_2E_2$, $\rho_s = D_{2n} - D_{1n}$; insulator + leaky: all $V$ on the insulator.
  • $\rho = \rho_0e^{-t/\tau}$, then integrate $\partial_zE_z = \rho/\epsilon$. Conductor: $\mathbf E = 0$, $\rho = 0$ inside.

7 · Boundaries and materials

  • $E_t$, $B_n$ continuous; $D_n$ jumps $\rho_s$, $H_t$ jumps $J_s$. With no $\rho_s$, $J_s$: tangential $D$, $B$ and normal $E$, $H$ scale by the $\epsilon$ or $\mu$ ratio. $\frac{\tan\theta_2}{\tan\theta_1} = \frac{\mu_2}{\mu_1}$.
  • $\mathbf H$: free currents only. $\mathbf M = (\mu_r - 1)\mathbf H$, $\mathbf B = \mu_0(\mathbf H + \mathbf M)$, bound sheet $\mathbf M\times\hat n_{\text{out}}$; $\mathbf P = (\epsilon - \epsilon_0)\mathbf E$. Dia $\chi_m < 0$, para small $> 0$, ferro $\gg 1$.

8 · Displacement current, curls, A

  • $\mathbf J_d = \partial\mathbf D/\partial t$; gap current $C\dot V$ = wire current. Round plates: $H_\phi = \tfrac r2\epsilon\dot E$ in, $\tfrac{R^2}{2r}\epsilon\dot E$ out. $J_c/J_d = \sigma/\omega\epsilon$.
  • $\nabla\times[f(w)\hat e] = f'(w)\,\hat w\times\hat e$. Curl amplitude $\beta\times$ amplitude, then $\div\mu\omega$ or $\epsilon\omega$: $E_0 = \eta H_0$, $\hat{\mathbf H} = \hat{\mathbf k}\times\hat{\mathbf E}$.
  • $\mathbf B = \nabla\times\mathbf A$, $\mathbf E = -\nabla\Phi - \partial\mathbf A/\partial t$, $\Psi = \oint\mathbf A\cdot d\mathbf l$, $\mathbf A + \nabla\psi$ same $\mathbf B$; check $\nabla\cdot\mathbf B = 0$; $\mathbf J = \nabla\times\mathbf B/\mu_0$.

9 · Waves: read and sketch

  • $v = \frac\omega\beta = f\lambda = \frac{c}{\sqrt{\mu_r\epsilon_r}}$, $\eta = \eta_0\sqrt{\frac{\mu_r}{\epsilon_r}}$, $\lambda = \frac{2\pi}\beta$.
  • Sheet: $\mathbf E = -\tfrac\eta2\mathbf J_s(t - \frac{|z|}{v})$ both sides; $|H| = \frac{J_s}{2}$, $\mathbf E\times\mathbf H$ away; $H$ flips across, $E$ doesn't.
  • Field at $(z, t)$ = source at $t - |z|/v$; front at $|z| = vt$.
  • vs $t$: source shape, delayed. vs $z$: reversed, first part farthest out. Travel $-z$: $E(z,t) = E(0, t + z/v)$.
  • Phase change $\Delta\phi$ in time $\Delta t$ at one point: $\omega = \Delta\phi/\Delta t$, then $\lambda = 2\pi v/\omega$. Over a distance $\Delta z$ at one time: $\beta = \Delta\phi/\Delta z$.
  • Valid wave: depends on $t \mp z/v$ only, with $v = 1/\sqrt{\mu\epsilon}$; $\cos\omega t\cos\beta z$ is two waves (standing).

Ex 1 · Diagonal voltmeter

Square 2 m, $\dot B_z = +3$ T/s; $R_1 = R_2 = R_3 = 2\,\Omega$ (left, top, right). + lead at A.

  • $\mathcal E_{ccw} = -(4)(3) = -12$ V; $I = -2$ A: 2 A clockwise (Lenz ✓).
  • Diagonal: lower-right triangle holds half: $V + IR_3 = \mathcal E/2$ → $V = -6 + 4 = -2$ V.
  • Leads hugging $R_3$: $V = -IR_3 = +4$ V.

Ex 2 · Loop in a graded field

$s = 0.5$ m, $\mathbf B = 0.2(1 + x/2)\hat z$ T, $\mathbf v = 4\hat x$ m/s.

  • $\Psi = s^2B(x_c)$ → $\mathcal E = -s^2B_0v/L = -0.1$ V: clockwise (into stronger flux).
  • Check: $vs\,\Delta B = 4(0.5)(0.05) = 0.1$ V ✓.

Ex 3 · Bar on rails

$B = 0.5$ T, $\ell = 0.4$ m, $v = 5$ m/s, $R = 2\,\Omega$.

  • $\mathcal E = vB\ell = 1$ V, $I = 0.5$ A, $F = I\ell B = 0.1$ N against $\mathbf v$.
  • $P = Fv = 0.5$ W $= I^2R$ ✓.

Ex 4 · Slab + sheet

$\mathbf J = 3\hat x\,[u(z + 1) - u(z - 1)] + 2\hat x\,\delta(z - 2)$.

  • Slab: $H_y = -3z$ in, $\mp3$ out. Sheet: $\tfrac12(2\hat x)\times(\pm\hat z) = \mp\hat y$.
  • $H_y(0) = 1$, $H_y(1.5) = -2$, $H_y(3) = -4$, $H_y(-2) = 4$ A/m. Net $K = 8$: far $\pm4$ ✓.

Ex 5 · Coax: L′, C′, v

$b/a = 4$, filling $\epsilon_r = 2.25$.

  • $L' = 2\times10^{-7}\ln4 = 0.277\ \mu$H/m; $C' = \frac{2\pi\epsilon}{\ln4} = 90.3$ pF/m.
  • $L'C' = 2.5\times10^{-17} = \epsilon_r/c^2$ ✓, $v = 2\times10^8$ m/s.

Ex 6 · PP line and toroid; scaling

  • $w = 5$ cm, $d = 1$ mm, $\ell = 2$ m: $L = \mu_0d\ell/w = 50.3$ nH. $d\times2$, $I\times\tfrac12$: $B\times\tfrac12$, $\Psi\times1$, $L\times2$.
  • Toroid $N = 200$, $h = 2$ cm, $a = 1$, $b = 3$ cm: $L = 2\times10^{-7}(4\times10^4)(0.02)\ln3 = 0.176$ mH; iron core $\mu_r = 100$: ×100.

Ex 7 · Two layers, then a leak

Air 1 mm under $\epsilon_r = 4$, 2 mm; 9 V.

  • $D = \frac{9}{10^{-3}/\epsilon_0 + 2\times10^{-3}/4\epsilon_0} = 6000\epsilon_0 = 5.31\times10^{-8}$ C/m².
  • $E_1 = 6000$, $E_2 = 1500$ V/m (6 + 3 = 9 V ✓); $P_2 = \tfrac34D = 3.98\times10^{-8}$.
  • Top layer leaks: $E_2 = 0$, $E_1 = 9000$ V/m, interface $\rho_s = 9000\epsilon_0 = 7.97\times10^{-8}$ C/m².

Ex 8 · Lossy coax; relaxation

  • $G' = 5\ \mu$S/m, $\sigma = 10^{-5}$, $\epsilon_r = 4$: $C' = \epsilon G'/\sigma = 17.7$ pF/m, $\tau = \epsilon/\sigma = 3.54\ \mu$s. 10 V through $R_s = 1/G$: 5 V.
  • $\rho(z,0) = \cos2z$: $\rho = \cos(2z)e^{-t/\tau}$, $E_z = \frac{\sin2z}{2\epsilon}e^{-t/\tau}$.

Ex 9 · Pulse from a sheet

$\mathbf J_s = -J(t)\hat x$, triangle 0 → 2 A/m at 1 ns → 0 at 2 ns; free space, $v = 0.3$ m/ns.

  • $z = 0.45$ m, $t = 2$ ns: $\tau = 2 - 1.5 = 0.5$ ns, $J = 1$: $E_x = 60\pi = 188$ V/m, $H_y = +0.5$ A/m, $\mathbf S$ along $+\hat z$.
  • Snapshot at 2 ns: pulse in $|z| < 0.6$ m, peak at $|z| = 0.3$ m.

Ex 10 · Read a sinusoid

$\mathbf E = 10\cos(6\pi\times10^8t - 4\pi z)\hat x$.

  • $f = 300$ MHz, $\lambda = 0.5$ m, $v = 1.5\times10^8$: $\epsilon_r = 4$, $\eta = 60\pi$.
  • $\mathbf H = 0.0531\cos(\cdot)\hat y$ ($\hat z\times\hat x = \hat y$); $\langle S\rangle = \frac{100}{120\pi} = 0.265$ W/m².

Ex 11 · H between capacitor plates

$R = 4$ cm, $d = 2$ mm, $V = 50\sin(2\pi\times10^6t)$.

  • $\epsilon_0\dot E_{\max} = \epsilon_0\frac{V_0\omega}{d} = 1.39$ A/m².
  • $H(2\ \text{cm}) = \tfrac r2(1.39) = 0.0139$; $H(8\ \text{cm}) = \frac{R^2}{2r}(1.39) = 0.0139$ A/m; $I_0 = 6.99$ mA.

Ex 12 · Magnetic slab; A to J

  • Sheets $\pm5$ A/m, gap filled with $\mu_r = 20$: $H = 5$ A/m (as in air), $B = 100\mu_0 = 1.26\times10^{-4}$ T, $M = 95$ A/m, bound sheets $\pm95$ A/m.
  • $\mathbf A = -k|x|\hat y$: $\nabla\cdot\mathbf A = 0$; $\mathbf B = \hat z\,\partial_xA_y = -k\operatorname{sgn}(x)\hat z$; jump at 0: $\mathbf J_s = \frac{2k}{\mu_0}\hat y$.

Power

  • $\mathbf S = \mathbf E\times\mathbf H$ (W/m²), $S = E^2/\eta = \eta H^2$; $\langle S\rangle = \frac{E_0^2}{2\eta} = \tfrac12\eta H_0^2$; $P = SA\cos\theta$; energy per area $\int S\,dt$.
  • $\langle w\rangle = \tfrac12\epsilon E_0^2$ ($w_e = w_m$), $S = wv$. A sheet supplies $\frac\eta2J_s^2$ per m² ($\frac\eta4J_s^2$ each way); $\mathbf E\cdot\mathbf J_s < 0$ there.

Traps

  • cm² → m²: $\times10^{-4}$. $\omega = 2\pi f$. $\ln$, not log. $\mathcal E = -N\dot\Psi_1$, but $L \propto N^2$.
  • $\tau = \epsilon/\sigma$ (Summer 2017 key: upside down). A big constant $B$ gives no emf.
  • Sheet: $\eta/2$ and $|z|$. Average power: the ½. $H_t$ is continuous across a $\mu$ change, $B_t$ is not.
