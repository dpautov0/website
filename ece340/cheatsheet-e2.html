0 · Knob → what moves (p⁺-n: the light side is n)

  • Light-side $N$ ↑: $V_0$ ↑ (+60 mV/decade), $W$ ↓ ($N^{-1/2}$), $\mathscr{E}_0$ ↑ ($N^{1/2}$), $C_j$ ↑ ($N^{1/2}$), $I_0$ ↓ ($1/N$), $V_{br}$ ↓ ($1/N$, toward Zener).
  • Heavy-side $N$ ↑: only $V_0$ moves (+60 mV/decade). $W$, $C_j$, $I_0$, $V_{br}$ ≈ same.
  • Reverse $V_r$ ↑: $W$, $\mathscr{E}_0 \propto \sqrt{V_0 + V_r}$ ↑; $C_j$ ↓; $I \approx -I_0$, flat.
  • Forward $V$ ↑: $W$, $\mathscr{E}_0$ ↓; $C_j$ ↑; $I$, $\Delta p_n$, $Q_p$, $C_s$ ×10 per 60 mV.
  • $T$ ↑: $n_i^2$ ↑↑ → $I_0$ ↑ (~15%/K); $V$ at fixed $I$ ↓ ~2 mV/K; $V_0$ ↓; $V_{oc}$ ↓. Avalanche $V_{br}$ ↑, Zener $V_{br}$ ↓.
  • $E_g$ ↑: $n_i$ ↓↓ → $V_0$ ↑, $I_0$ ↓↓, $V_{oc}$ ↑, LED $\lambda$ ↓.
  • $\tau_p$ ↑: $L_p$ ↑ (√), $I_0$ ↓ ($1/\sqrt{\tau}$), $Q_p$, $C_s$, $t_{sd}$ ↑ (∝ $\tau$).
  • Area ↑: $I_0$, $I$, $C_j$, $C_s$, $I_{sc}$ ∝ $A$. $V_0$, $W$, $\mathscr{E}$, $V_{oc}$ unchanged.
  • Light ×10: $I_{sc}$ ×10, $V_{oc}$ +60 mV. Narrow base $\ell$ ↓: $I \propto 1/\ell$ ↑, $t_t \propto \ell^2$ ↓.

1 · Haynes–Shockley, quasi-Fermi levels

  • Peak drifts: $\mu_p = \frac{d}{\mathscr{E}t_d}$. Spreads: $\Delta x = v_d\Delta t$, $D_p = \frac{(\Delta x)^2}{16t_d}$. Area ∝ $e^{-t/\tau_p}$.
  • $\Delta x \propto \sqrt{t_d}$. Check $D/\mu = kT/q$.
  • $n = n_ie^{(F_n - E_i)/kT}$, $p = n_ie^{(E_i - F_p)/kT}$, $np = n_i^2e^{(F_n - F_p)/kT}$.
  • Low level: the majority level barely moves; the minority level moves a lot.
  • $J_n = \mu_nn\frac{dF_n}{dx}$: current needs a sloped quasi-Fermi level.

2 · Contact potential

  • $V_0 = \frac{kT}{q}\ln\frac{N_aN_d}{n_i^2}$ = (p side $E_i - E_F$) + (n side $E_F - E_i$).
  • Si: $V_0 = 0.0596(\log N_a + \log N_d - 20.35)$.
  • $E_F$ flat; bands bend by $qV_0$; $\frac{p_p}{p_n} = \frac{n_n}{n_p} = e^{qV_0/kT}$.
  • Drift = diffusion for each carrier separately; each is large.
  • Typical Si: 0.6–0.9 V; always $< E_g/q$.

3 · Depletion region

  • $N_ax_{p0} = N_dx_{n0}$: the light side holds most of $W$. $x_{n0} = W\frac{N_a}{N_a + N_d}$.
  • $W = \sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon(V_0 - V)}{q}\left(\frac1{N_a} + \frac1{N_d}\right)}$; one-sided $\sqrt{\frac{2\epsilon(V_0 - V)}{qN_{light}}}$.
  • $\mathscr{E}_0 = \frac{qN_dx_{n0}}{\epsilon} = \frac{2(V_0 - V)}{W}$, peak at the junction, points n → p. Triangle area = $V_0 - V$.
  • $\frac{V_n}{V_p} = \frac{x_{n0}}{x_{p0}} = \frac{N_a}{N_d}$: the light side drops most of it.
  • Mobile carriers in $W$: essentially none.

4 · Bias, law of the junction

  • Barrier → $V_0 - V$. Across $W$: $F_n - F_p = qV$.
  • Edges: $\Delta p_n = p_n(e^{qV/kT} - 1)$, $\Delta n_p = n_p(e^{qV/kT} - 1)$. Reverse: $\to -p_n$.
  • Long side: $\delta p = \Delta p_ne^{-x_n/L_p}$, $L_p = \sqrt{D_p\tau_p}$.
  • Low level needs $\Delta p_n \ll N_d$.

5 · Diode current and its components

  • $I = I_0(e^{qV/kT} - 1)$, $I_0 = qA\left(\frac{D_p}{L_p}p_n + \frac{D_n}{L_n}n_p\right)$.
  • Forward: majority carriers cross the lowered barrier and become injected minority carriers. Reverse: minority carriers within $L$ of $W$ are swept across.
  • The light side's minority carriers carry it (p⁺-n: holes).
  • $I_p(x_n) = I_p(0)e^{-x_n/L_p}$; the total is constant; far away it is majority drift, $\mathscr{E} = \frac{J}{q\mu_nN_d}$ (tiny).
  • ×10 per 60 mV. $e^{qV/nkT}$: $n \approx 2$ at low $I$ (recombination in $W$) and at high $I$ (high level); series $R$ bends the top.
  • Real reverse current grows with $V_r$: generation in $W$, ∝ $W$.

6 · Narrow base ($\ell \ll L_p$)

  • Straight line, $\Delta p = 0$ at the contact. $I = qAD_p\frac{\Delta p_n}{\ell}$: $L_p \to \ell$, more current.
  • $Q_p = \frac12qA\ell\Delta p_n$, $t_t = \frac{Q_p}{I} = \frac{\ell^2}{2D_p} \ll \tau_p$: fast.
  • Base drop $V_{base} \approx \frac{kT}{q}\frac{\Delta p_n}{N_d}$: an electron field cancels electron diffusion.

7 · Breakdown

  • $V_{br} = \frac{\epsilon\mathscr{E}_{crit}^2}{2qN}$ ∝ $1/N$; $W_{br} = \frac{2V_{br}}{\mathscr{E}_{crit}}$.
  • Zener (tunneling): heavy doping on both sides, $V_{br} < 4E_g/q \approx 4.5$ V, TC negative.
  • Avalanche (impact ionization): $V_{br} > 6E_g/q \approx 6.7$ V, TC positive. Between: both, TC ≈ 0.
  • $M = \frac{1}{1 - P}$; $P \to 1$ is breakdown. APD uses $M$ as gain.

8 · Capacitance and switching

  • $C_j = \frac{\epsilon A}{W} = A\sqrt{\frac{q\epsilon}{2(V_0 - V)}\frac{N_aN_d}{N_a + N_d}}$. Dominates in reverse.
  • $\frac{1}{C_j^2} = \frac{2(V_0 + V_r)}{q\epsilon NA^2}$: slope → $N_{light}$, intercept at $V_r = -V_0$.
  • $Q_p = I\tau_p$; $C_s = \frac{q}{kT}I\tau_p = G_s\tau_p$; $r = \frac{kT}{qI}$ (26 Ω at 1 mA). Dominates in forward.
  • Turn-off: $I$ flips to $-I_r$ and holds; $V$ stays forward until $\Delta p_n = 0$. $t_{sd} \approx \tau_p\ln\left(1 + \frac{I_f}{I_r}\right)$.
  • Faster: short $\tau_p$ (Au doping), narrow base, Schottky.

9 · Metal–semiconductor

  • n: $\phi_s = \chi + kT\ln\frac{N_c}{N_d}$. p: $\phi_s = \chi + E_g - kT\ln\frac{N_v}{N_a}$.
  • n-type: $\phi_m > \phi_s$ rectifying, $\phi_m < \phi_s$ ohmic. p-type: the reverse.
  • n: $\phi_B = \phi_m - \chi$ (from the metal, bias can't move it); $V_0 = \phi_m - \phi_s$ (from the Si, bias lowers it). p: $\phi_B = \chi + E_g - \phi_m$.
  • Majority-carrier device: no stored charge, fast, lower turn-on. Practical ohmic: dope the surface heavily so carriers tunnel.

10 · Light: solar cell, photodiode, LED

  • $I = I_{th}(e^{qV/kT} - 1) - I_{op}$, $I_{op} = qAg_{op}(L_p + L_n + W)$.
  • $I_{sc} = I_{op}$; $V_{oc} = \frac{kT}{q}\ln\left(1 + \frac{I_{op}}{I_{th}}\right)$.
  • $P_{max} = FF\cdot I_{sc}V_{oc}$, FF ≈ 0.7–0.85. Solar cell: 4th quadrant. Photodiode: 3rd (reverse), $I \propto$ light.
  • PIN: wide i-region → collects more, low $C$, fast. APD: gain $M$.
  • LED: $\lambda(\mu\text{m}) = 1.24/E_g(\text{eV})$, needs a direct gap. Laser: inversion ($F_n - F_p > h\nu$), a cavity, threshold current.

Ex 1 · Junction at equilibrium

$N_a = 4\times10^{17}$, $N_d = 2\times10^{16}$.

  • $V_0 = 0.0259\ln\frac{(4\times10^{17})(2\times10^{16})}{2.25\times10^{20}} = 0.808$ V.
  • $W = \sqrt{\frac{2(1.044\times10^{-12})(0.808)}{1.6\times10^{-19}}\left(\frac{1}{4\times10^{17}} + \frac{1}{2\times10^{16}}\right)} = 0.235\,\mu$m; $x_{n0} = 0.224\,\mu$m, $x_{p0} = 11.2$ nm.
  • $\mathscr{E}_0 = 2V_0/W = 6.87\times10^4$ V/cm; $V_n = V_0\frac{N_a}{N_a + N_d} = 0.770$ V.
  • 5 V reverse: $W \times \sqrt{5.808/0.808} = 0.631\,\mu$m; $\mathscr{E}_0 = 1.84\times10^5$ V/cm.

Ex 2 · Haynes–Shockley

$\mathscr{E} = 2$ V/cm, probe at 0.25 cm, $t_d = 260\,\mu$s, $\Delta t = 237\,\mu$s.

  • $v_d = 0.25/260\,\mu\text{s} = 960$ cm/s → $\mu_p = 480$ cm²/V·s.
  • $\Delta x = 960(237\times10^{-6}) = 0.228$ cm; $D_p = \frac{0.228^2}{16(260\times10^{-6})} = 12.4$ cm²/s; $D/\mu = 0.0259$ ✓.

Ex 3 · Edge carriers and quasi-Fermi levels

n side $N_d = 5\times10^{15}$, $V = 0.55$ V.

  • $p_n = 4.5\times10^4$; $e^{0.55/0.0259} = 1.67\times10^9$; $\Delta p_n = 7.51\times10^{13}$ (1.5% of $N_d$: low level).
  • $F_n - E_i = 0.0259\ln\frac{5\times10^{15}}{1.5\times10^{10}} = 0.329$ eV; $E_i - F_p = 0.55 - 0.329 = 0.221$ eV.

Ex 4 · Diode current and components

p⁺-n, $10^{18}/10^{16}$, $A = 10^{-4}$ cm². n: $D_p = 10$, $\tau_p = 1\,\mu$s, $\mu_n = 1250$. p: $D_n = 6$, $\tau_n = 0.1\,\mu$s. $V = 0.6$ V.

  • $L_p = 31.6\,\mu$m, $L_n = 7.75\,\mu$m. $I_0 = qA\left(\frac{10(2.25\times10^4)}{3.16\times10^{-3}} + \frac{6(225)}{7.75\times10^{-4}}\right) = 1.17\times10^{-15}$ A.
  • $I = I_0(1.15\times10^{10}) = 13.4\,\mu$A; holes $\frac{7.12}{7.29} = 97.6\%$.
  • Far field $\frac{J}{q\mu_nN_d} = \frac{0.134}{2.0} = 0.067$ V/cm. For 10 mA: $0.0259\ln\frac{10^{-2}}{I_0} = 0.771$ V.

Ex 5 · Narrow base

$\ell = 5\,\mu$m, $N_d = 10^{16}$, $D_p = 10$, $A = 10^{-4}$ cm², 0.55 V.

  • $\Delta p_n = 2.25\times10^4(1.67\times10^9) = 3.76\times10^{13}$.
  • $I = qAD_p\frac{\Delta p_n}{\ell} = 12.0\,\mu$A; $Q_p = \frac12qA\ell\Delta p_n = 1.50\times10^{-13}$ C.
  • $t_t = \frac{\ell^2}{2D_p} = 12.5$ ns; $V_{base} = (25.9\text{ mV})(0.00376) = 97\,\mu$V.

Ex 6 · Breakdown

  • p⁺-n, $N_d = 10^{17}$, $\mathscr{E}_{crit} = 5\times10^5$: $V_{br} = \frac{(1.044\times10^{-12})(2.5\times10^{11})}{2(1.6\times10^{-19})(10^{17})} = 8.2$ V. Avalanche, just above 6.7 V.
  • $N_d = 10^{15}$, $\mathscr{E}_{crit} = 3\times10^5$: $V_{br} = 294$ V; $W_{br} = \frac{2V_{br}}{\mathscr{E}_{crit}} = 19.6\,\mu$m.

Ex 7 · Junction capacitance

p⁺-n, $10^{18}/10^{16}$, $A = 10^{-3}$ cm².

  • $V_0 = 0.814$ V. $C_j(0) = A\sqrt{\frac{q\epsilon}{2V_0}\frac{N_aN_d}{N_a + N_d}} = 31.9$ pF.
  • −5 V: $\times\sqrt{0.814/5.814} = 11.9$ pF. +0.4 V: $\times\sqrt{0.814/0.414} = 44.7$ pF.

Ex 8 · Stored charge and turn-off

$\tau_p = 1.5\,\mu$s, $I_f = 2$ mA, switched to $I_r = 1$ mA.

  • $Q_p = I\tau_p = 3.0$ nC; $C_s = \frac{Q_p}{kT/q} = 116$ nF; $r = \frac{kT}{qI} = 12.9\,\Omega$.
  • $t_{sd} = 1.5\ln(1 + 2) = 1.65\,\mu$s.

Ex 9 · Two metals on n-Si

$N_d = 10^{17}$, $\chi = 4.05$ eV.

  • $E_c - E_F = 0.0259\ln280 = 0.146$ eV; $\phi_s = 4.196$ eV.
  • $\phi_m = 4.6 > \phi_s$: rectifying, $\phi_B = 0.55$ eV, $V_0 = 0.40$ V.
  • $\phi_m = 4.0 < \phi_s$: ohmic (electrons pile up at the surface).

Ex 10 · Solar cell

$A = 2$ cm², $g_{op} = 2\times10^{19}$, $L_p + L_n + W = 46\,\mu$m, $I_{th} = 10^{-11}$ A.

  • $I_{sc} = qAg_{op}(46\times10^{-4}) = 29.4$ mA; $V_{oc} = 0.0259\ln(2.94\times10^9) = 0.565$ V.
  • $V_{mp} \approx 0.487$ V: $P_{max} = 13.6$ mW, FF = 0.82. ×10 light: $V_{oc} = 0.624$ V.

Ex 11 · Light, fast

  • GaN LED: $1.24/3.4 = 0.365\,\mu$m (UV). InP: $1.24/1.35 = 0.919\,\mu$m (IR).
  • APD with $P = 0.75$: $M = \frac{1}{0.25} = 4$.
